### 新论文揭示 Agent「主动性」盲区：该问却没问

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.37236
- **TitleEN**：New Study Maps the Blind Spots in Agent Proactivity

研究指出现有 Agent 只回答用户明确提出的问题，却常常漏掉完成任务所需的隐含信息。论文把「主动性」拆成横向（追问当前场景已暗示的信息）和纵向（挖掘早期证据揭示的隐藏需求）两个维度，为设计更懂用户意图的 Agent 提供了新框架。对开发者来说，这意味着未来评估 Agent 好坏不能只看「答得对不对」，还要看「问得够不够」。

- **EN**：This paper argues that today's tool-using agents only answer what users explicitly ask, often missing implicit information needed to actually finish the task. It splits 'proactivity' into horizontal (following up on hints already in context) and vertical (surfacing needs revealed by earlier evidence) — a useful framework for developers building agents that don't just answer correctly, but ask the right follow-up questions.
