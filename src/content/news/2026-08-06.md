It solves state drift and goal loss in long-running multi-agent tasks—a practical layer for anyone running complex, multi-session coding workflows.

### ReflectRL：让推理模型从「失败轨迹」中也能学到东西

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.03972
- **TitleEN**：ReflectRL: learning from failed reasoning traces, not just successes

现有推理模型训练只用成功轨迹做监督，专家模型在难题上失败时这些数据就被丢弃。ReflectRL 提出「黄金负样本轨迹」概念，通过反思式推理将失败过程转化为可学习信号。对开发者意义：训练数据利用率更高，在困难任务上模型鲁棒性提升，尤其适合数据稀缺场景。

- **EN**：ReflectRL introduces 'Golden Negative Trajectories'—failed reasoning traces from expert models that are normally discarded. By converting failures into reflective-to-direct learning signals, it improves model robustness on hard tasks. For developers fine-tuning reasoning models: higher data efficiency and better performance where correct examples are scarce.
