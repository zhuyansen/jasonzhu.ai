### Microsoft Build 发布 MAI-Thinking-1 + MAI 自研模型家族

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：Latent Space
- **链接**：https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-microsoft-build-mai-thinking
- **TitleEN**：Microsoft Build Launches MAI-Thinking-1 and In-House Model Family

微软在 Build 大会一口气发布 **MAI-Thinking-1**（推理模型）和 **MAI Family** 自研模型家族。这是微软从「OpenAI 的最大客户兼渠道商」彻底转向「自己造模型」的标志性事件——和 Apple 自研、Amazon Nova、Meta Llama 一起，**「巨头自研派」正式形成完整闭环**。Anthropic / OpenAI 的纯模型公司护城河被进一步稀释。

- **EN**：Microsoft unveiled MAI-Thinking-1, a reasoning model, alongside the broader MAI family of proprietary models at Build 2025. This marks Microsoft's definitive shift from OpenAI's biggest reseller to an independent model builder. Joining Apple, Amazon Nova, and Meta Llama, the 'big-tech builds its own' camp is now a complete circle, further eroding the moat of pure-play model companies like Anthropic and OpenAI.

### Lovable 与 Google Cloud 签多年协议，算力上调 5 倍

- **板块**：变现案例
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/lovable-signs-multi-year-deal-with-google-cloud-to-up-usage-5x-source-says/
- **TitleEN**：Lovable Signs Multi-Year Google Cloud Deal, 5x Compute

Vibe Coding 头部 Lovable 与 Google Cloud 签下多年大单，**算力使用量直接上调 5 倍**——意味着用户增长把基础设施压力推到了「必须长期锁定供应商」的级别。对独立开发者：Vibe Coding 赛道的资本/算力门槛正在快速抬高，留给新玩家的窗口越来越窄。

- **EN**：Vibe Coding leader Lovable has signed a multi-year agreement with Google Cloud, scaling compute usage by five times. The deal signals that user growth has pushed infrastructure pressure to a level requiring long-term vendor commitment. For independent developers, the capital and compute barriers in the Vibe Coding space are rising fast, leaving an ever-narrowing window for new entrants.

Using AI to accelerate comprehension is not the same as letting AI comprehend for you.

新监管允许出版商**主动 opt out 被 AI Search 摘要**——意味着新闻媒体可以拒绝 Google AI Overviews、Perplexity 等 AI 搜索引擎抓取内容做总结。对所有依赖「白嫖优质内容做 RAG」的 AI 产品：合规风险正在从可选变必选，内容来源审查要提上 roadmap。
