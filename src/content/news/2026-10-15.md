### DuplexJev让语音智能体无需解码即可快速决策

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02638
- **TitleEN**：DuplexJev Enables Fast Speech Decisions Without Decoding

DuplexJev把语音编码器信息接入冻结的大语言模型，用单个Token直接判断语音对话中的封闭选项，不再逐字生成答案。实验显示，8张GPU可在约0.1秒完成80次决策，有望显著降低实时语音Agent延迟与成本。

- **EN**：DuplexJev connects ASR representations to a frozen language model and makes closed-set decisions through a single-token distribution instead of autoregressive decoding. It reportedly handles 80 decisions for eight utterances in about 0.1 seconds on eight GPUs, offering a practical path to faster and cheaper full-duplex voice agents.

### WorkForge自动生成可验证的长任务训练环境

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.04906
- **TitleEN**：WorkForge Builds Verifiable Long-Horizon Agent Environments

WorkForge提出可规模化合成训练环境，让工作型Agent在真实数字工作区中完成长链任务，并自动验证产出是否正确。它针对手工搭建环境成本高、合成数据难验证的问题，为训练能处理专业文档和业务流程的Agent提供基础设施。

- **EN**：WorkForge introduces a framework for synthesizing scalable training environments where work agents interact with realistic digital workspaces and receive verifiable outcome checks. It addresses the high cost of hand-built environments and the weak grounding of synthetic data, helping developers train agents for complex professional workflows.

### 研究发现工具调用评测存在隐藏的推导鸿沟

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18497
- **TitleEN**：Study Finds a Derivation Gap in Tool-Call Evaluation

研究发现，评估Agent工具调用的模型在看不到明确参考文本时，更容易错误拒绝本来正确的参数，尤其涉及默认值或上下文推断。开发者需要为评测器提供完整依据，否则工具调用准确率可能被系统性低估。

- **EN**：The study finds that tool-call judges are more likely to reject correct arguments when the supporting reference is absent, especially for schema defaults or values inferred from context. Developers should provide complete evidence to evaluators, or agent tool-use accuracy may be systematically underestimated.
