### OpenAI 模型独立推翻 80 年 Erdős 猜想：AI 数学推理迎来里程碑

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：OpenAI Blog
- **链接**：https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture
- **TitleEN**：OpenAI Model Independently Solves 80-Year-Old Erdős Conjecture

OpenAI 最新推理模型自主解决了困扰数学界 80 年的单位距离问题（Erdős 猜想），借助 Golod-Shafarevich 理论将离散几何与数论深度连接，耗费不到 1000 美元算力。成果已获 Noga Alon、Tim Gowers 等顶级数学家验证，被称为「AI 数学里程碑」。

- **EN**：OpenAI's latest reasoning model autonomously resolved the unit distance problem, an Erdős conjecture that stumped mathematicians for 80 years, by deeply connecting discrete geometry and number theory via Golod-Shafarevich theory — all for under $1,000 in compute. Top mathematicians including Noga Alon and Tim Gowers have verified the result, calling it an AI math milestone.

### Stable Audio 3 论文：快速扩散模型家族，支持分钟级可变长度音频生成与编辑

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.17991
- **TitleEN**：Stable Audio 3 Enables Minute-Long Variable-Length Audio Generation

Stability AI 发布 Stable Audio 3 系列（小/中/大三档），基于新型语义-声学自编码器的潜扩散架构，可生成数分钟可变长度音频并支持局部修复式编辑。对开发者的意义：无需按最长时长生成整段音频，短声效也能低成本生成，音频应用集成成本大幅降低。

- **EN**：Stability AI's Stable Audio 3 family — available in small, medium, and large variants — uses a novel semantic-acoustic autoencoder latent diffusion architecture to generate minutes of variable-length audio and support localized inpainting-style editing. Developers benefit from not needing to generate full-length audio for short sound effects, significantly lowering integration costs for audio applications.
