### Know then Draw重构视觉生成训练路线

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.37626
- **TitleEN**：Know Then Draw Reworks Visual Generation

论文提出“先理解、后绘制”的训练范式：先用自回归模型学习语义知识，再用扩散模型负责高保真渲染。对开发者而言，这提示视觉模型可以拆分为知识理解与像素生成两阶段，以兼顾复杂语义和画面质量。

- **EN**：The paper proposes a “know, then draw” training paradigm: autoregressive pretraining acquires semantic knowledge, while diffusion post-training handles high-fidelity rendering. For developers, the result suggests separating understanding from pixel generation can improve both complex prompt comprehension and visual quality.

### 研究量化大模型同时遵循多少条指令

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36095
- **TitleEN**：Study Measures Language Models’ Instruction Capacity

研究构造可计算答案的独立规则任务，测量模型在指令数量增加时的遵循能力及退化边界。对开发者来说，这有助于解释长系统提示为何会失效，也提醒产品应拆分规则、减少冲突并做压力测试。

- **EN**：The study introduces controlled tasks with independent, verifiable rules to measure how instruction following degrades as rule load increases. For developers, it provides a practical way to understand why long system prompts fail and why policies should be modularized, deconflicted, and stress-tested.

### REALCLI补上CLI智能体协作评测空白

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22507
- **TitleEN**：REALCLI Benchmarks Human-Agent CLI Collaboration

REALCLI提出面向真实命令行任务的评测基准，同时衡量任务是否完成和人机协作质量，不再假设用户指令全程不变。它能帮助开发者评估Agent如何接受中途反馈、调整计划并与用户共同完成长期任务。

- **EN**：REALCLI is a benchmark for realistic command-line tasks that evaluates both execution outcomes and human-agent collaboration, rather than assuming fixed instructions. It helps developers assess whether agents can accept runtime feedback, revise plans, communicate effectively, and complete longer-horizon tasks with users.
