### AutoMem：让LLM把记忆管理变成可训练技能的新框架

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.01224
- **TitleEN**：AutoMem: Teaching LLMs Memory Management as a Trainable Cognitive Skill

AutoMem将认知科学中「元记忆」概念引入LLM训练：让模型自主决定何时存、何时取、如何组织信息，把文件系统操作提升为与任务动作同等地位的「一等记忆动作」。对开发者的意义：未来的Agent不需要你手写记忆调度逻辑，模型自己就能学会管理长期记忆。

- **EN**：AutoMem brings cognitive science's 'metamemory' concept to LLM training — letting models learn when to encode, retrieve, and organize information as a first-class trainable skill. Why it matters for developers: future agents could manage their own long-term memory without you hardcoding memory scheduling logic into your prompts or pipelines.
