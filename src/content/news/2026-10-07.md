### 安全警示：被「破解」的开源模型可窃取编码Agent凭证

- **板块**：Skills 生态
- **来源**：Hacker News
- **链接**：https://projectdiscovery.io/research/how-abliterated-models-can-get-you-pwned
- **TitleEN**：Backdoored open model can steal credentials from coding agents

研究人员展示了对「abliterated」（去安全对齐）开源模型植入后门后，可在编码 Agent 执行任务时窃取开发者凭证。提醒使用第三方开源模型驱动 Agent 时需警惕供应链安全风险。

- **EN**：Researchers demonstrate that backdoored 'abliterated' open-weight models can exfiltrate developer credentials when used inside coding agents. A warning for anyone plugging third-party open models into agentic coding workflows.

### 新论文证明：LLM本身就是「Jev」式分类决策模型

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02076
- **TitleEN**：New paper: LLMs already act as Jev-style decision models

论文发现通用 LLM 不需要微调，仅通过读取 next-token 概率就能变成可靠的分类/路由模型（如用于审核、分流任务），并给出何时才真正需要微调。对想用 LLM 做轻量分类器而不想训练专用模型的开发者很实用。

- **EN**：This paper shows general-purpose LLMs can already act as calibrated classification/routing models just by reading next-token probabilities — no fine-tuning required for many cases — and clarifies when fine-tuning actually helps. Useful for devs who want LLM-based classifiers without training a dedicated model.
