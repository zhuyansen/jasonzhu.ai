### OpenAI推出湿实验Agent基准测试

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：OpenAI Blog
- **链接**：https://openai.com/index/measuring-how-ai-agents-handle-wet-lab-science
- **TitleEN**：OpenAI Benchmarks AI Agents in Wet Labs

OpenAI发布BioBasicsBench，包含100项任务，用于评估AI Agent读取原始实验数据、理解实验流程并处理日常湿实验室工作的能力。它为科研Agent提供了更贴近真实工作的测评标准，也暴露出模型从文本推理走向可验证科学操作的差距。

- **EN**：OpenAI introduced BioBasicsBench, a 100-task benchmark for evaluating AI agents on everyday wet-lab science, including raw experimental data interpretation and experimental procedures. It gives developers a more realistic way to measure scientific agents beyond text-only reasoning, while highlighting the gap between understanding experiments and reliably supporting real laboratory workflows.

### 离线RAG签名知识包仍可能被向量劫持

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06498
- **TitleEN**：Signed RAG Packs Still Face Vector Hijacking

论文发现，离线RAG使用签名知识包时，签名只能证明文件来自可信发布者，却不能保证文本与预计算向量匹配。攻击者若篡改向量，可能操纵检索结果。对开发者的启示是：更新包校验不能只验签名，还要重新计算并核对Embedding。

- **EN**：The paper shows that signatures on offline RAG knowledge packs prove publisher identity but not whether embeddings actually match the signed text. An attacker controlling vectors may redirect retrieval toward malicious content. Developers deploying edge or offline RAG systems should verify both signatures and text-vector consistency instead of trusting signed metadata alone.

### SwarmEvolve用低成本Agent群加速算法搜索

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08483
- **TitleEN**：SwarmEvolve Speeds Search with Agent Swarms

SwarmEvolve让数百个低成本Agent以群体方式并行改进程序，并在潜在空间中推理，减少反复生成长链式思考文本的开销。它为数学和计算问题的自动优化提供了更高吞吐路径，开发者可借鉴其群体搜索思路构建代码优化与实验探索系统。

- **EN**：SwarmEvolve uses hundreds of inexpensive agents in parallel to evolve programs for mathematical and computational tasks. Instead of repeatedly producing long reasoning traces, agents search in latent space, reducing cost and latency. The approach suggests a practical architecture for high-throughput code optimization, experiment discovery, and automated algorithm search.

- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/hundreds-of-artex-ai-hacking-tools-found-publicly-exposed-to-the-web/
