- **来源**：GitHub Trending

- **TitleEN**：NVIDIA Open Sources Agent Skill Evaluation Framework

### 本地代码模型选型研究比较体积与解题能耗

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17605
- **TitleEN**：Study Compares Local Coding Models by Size and Energy

研究在无风扇 MacBook Air M2 上测试24个 Qwen2.5-Coder 量化文件，以542道任务衡量每次成功解题的能耗。对本地代码助手开发者，价值在于按下载体积与实际解题成本选模型，而非只看参数规模。

- **EN**：Researchers tested 24 quantized Qwen2.5-Coder GGUF files on a fanless MacBook Air M2 across 542 EvalPlus tasks, measuring energy per solved task. The resulting selection guide helps local coding-assistant developers compare download size and practical energy costs instead of relying solely on parameter counts or quantization rules of thumb.

### StatePortal生成检查点直达软件深层测试状态

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.23257
- **TitleEN**：StatePortal Synthesizes Checkpoints for Deep Software Testing

StatePortal 针对交互软件测试中的难题：目标状态往往需要长串操作才能到达。它让智能体生成检查点，旨在减少重复操作成本；对缺少标准界面结构的自绘软件，这提供了传统界面自动化之外的测试思路。

- **EN**：StatePortal addresses a testing bottleneck in interactive software: reaching states that require long interaction histories. Its agent synthesizes checkpoints intended to reduce repeated setup work across commits. This is especially relevant to developers of self-rendered interfaces, where conventional GUI automation cannot rely on a DOM or accessibility tree.
