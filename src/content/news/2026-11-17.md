### LuthorSQL让本地小模型接管Text-to-SQL

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17780
- **TitleEN**：LuthorSQL Offloads Text-to-SQL to Local Models

LuthorSQL提出一次性生成SQL的轻量模型，作为慢速大模型Agent的第一层处理器。开发者可将高频、简单的数据库查询转到本地硬件，降低API成本与数据外泄风险，同时保留复杂问题交给强模型。

- **EN**：LuthorSQL presents a one-pass text-to-SQL model that acts as a fast first layer before a slower frontier-model agent. For developers, this means routine database queries can run locally, cutting API costs and privacy exposure while reserving expensive models for difficult cases.

### Alloy把多材料物理模拟装进统一引擎

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.16312
- **TitleEN**：Alloy Unifies Multi-Material Physics for Robot Learning

Alloy开源机器人学习物理引擎，让刚体、布料、绳索、液体、颗粒和软体材料共存于同一场景，并由不同求解器协同处理。对开发者而言，这能减少多模拟器拼接和手写适配，提升复杂操作训练效率。

- **EN**：Alloy is an open-source physics engine that supports rigid bodies, cloth, cables, liquids, granular materials, and soft bodies in one scene. For robotics developers, it reduces the need to combine separate simulators and hand-build adapters, making complex manipulation training easier to scale.
