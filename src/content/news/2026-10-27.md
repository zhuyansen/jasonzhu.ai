### Kolab基准发现智能体协作胜过一次性自动完成

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.21109
- **TitleEN**：Kolab Benchmarks Human-Agent Collaboration

Kolab专门评估人类与智能体共同完成长期任务的能力。研究发现，专业用户更看重持续对话、修改计划和共同推进，而不是智能体一次性独立完成。对开发者来说，协作体验可能比自动化分数更重要。

- **EN**：Kolab evaluates human-agent collaboration on long-horizon workflows and finds that professionals value ongoing interaction, plan revision, and shared progress more than one-shot task completion. For developers, the result suggests that agent products should optimize for effective collaboration and recovery, not merely benchmarked automation rates.

### FLORA把机器人训练所需数据引擎全面开源

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31779
- **TitleEN**：FLORA Opens a Robotics Data Engine

FLORA是一套面向机器人基础模型的数据引擎，同时生成结果奖励、过程奖励和带时间戳的训练描述，并用于训练GR00T模型。它把机器人训练中分散、昂贵的数据标注流程标准化，降低开发门槛。

- **EN**：FLORA is an open model suite and data engine for robotics foundation models. It supports outcome rewards, process rewards, and timestamped captions for training and deployment, and has been used with GR00T models. By standardizing fragmented labeling workflows, it could reduce the data bottleneck facing robotics developers.

### LLM2Cert让大模型生成的安全证明交给Lean验算

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30524
- **TitleEN**：LLM2Cert Connects Language Models with Formal Verification

LLM2Cert让大模型尝试生成控制系统的稳定性与安全性证明，再交由SMT求解器或Lean进行形式化验证，避免只凭语言模型自我解释。对开发者而言，这为机器人和自动驾驶系统引入可检查的安全保证。

- **EN**：LLM2Cert uses language models to synthesize stability and safety certificates for control systems, then verifies them with SMT solvers or Lean. This avoids relying on plausible-sounding explanations alone and offers developers a path toward machine-checkable safety guarantees for robotics, autonomous systems, and other high-risk applications.
