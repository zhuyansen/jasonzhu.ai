### AdaPRA让数据科学Agent持续积累解题经验

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.33245
- **TitleEN**：AdaPRA Helps Data Science Agents Learn Across Attempts

AdaPRA为数据科学Agent加入持久化工作台、过程评分标准和跨尝试知识积累，针对重复EDA、验证不足和代码错误等问题进行改进。对开发者而言，关键价值是让Agent不只追求一次答对，而能复用经验并更有效分配预算。

- **EN**：AdaPRA adds a persistent coding harness, rubric-based process rewards, and knowledge accumulation across attempts for data science agents. It targets common failures such as shallow exploration, weak validation, coding bugs, and wasted budgets. For developers, the important idea is training agents to improve their workflow over repeated tasks, not merely optimize one-shot answers.

### SARL让RAG智能体知道还缺哪些信息

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30435
- **TitleEN**：SARL Teaches RAG Agents When Search Is Incomplete

SARL通过自我评估强化学习训练搜索Agent判断当前证据是否足够、还缺哪些信息，再决定继续检索还是作答。它针对RAG常见的过早停止和凭空补全问题，对构建研究、客服与知识库Agent尤其有参考价值。

- **EN**：SARL trains retrieval-augmented agents to assess what evidence they already have, identify what is still missing, and decide whether to search further or answer. This directly addresses premature stopping and hallucinated reasoning in RAG systems, offering a practical direction for research, support, and knowledge-base agents.
