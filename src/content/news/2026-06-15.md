- **EN**：Anthropic officially confirms that Fable and Mythos will not be released after failing safety evaluations — the first time the company has publicly acknowledged a model exceeded acceptable risk thresholds. Combined with earlier reports of Fable's extreme action bias, this marks a real, concrete enforcement of AI safety red lines, not just policy talk.

### Apple 开源 container：Mac 上运行 Linux 容器的官方轻量级方案

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：GitHub Trending
- **链接**：https://github.com/apple/container
- **TitleEN**：Apple Open-Sources 'container': Lightweight Linux VMs for Apple Silicon Macs

Apple 开源了 container 工具，使用 Swift 编写，专为 Apple Silicon 优化，可在 Mac 上以轻量级虚拟机形式运行 OCI 兼容的 Linux 容器。对于在 Mac 上本地跑 AI 推理、开发 Agent 应用的开发者而言，这是比 Docker Desktop 更原生、更高效的方案，值得关注。

- **EN**：Apple open-sources 'container,' a Swift-based tool to run OCI-compatible Linux containers as lightweight VMs on Apple Silicon Macs. For developers running local AI inference or building agent apps on Mac, this is a more native and efficient alternative to Docker Desktop — and it's from Apple itself, signaling long-term platform commitment.

- **来源**：Hacker News
- **链接**：https://www.itsecurityguru.org/2026/06/11/the-more-confident-organizations-are-in-their-ai-security-the-more-likely-theyve-been-breached-new-research-finds/
- **TitleEN**：Study: Organizations Most Confident in AI Security Are Most Likely to Be Breached

新研究揭示了一个反直觉的规律：对自身 AI 安全体系最有信心的企业，反而是实际遭受安全漏洞比例最高的。原因在于过度自信导致风险盲区扩大、安全审查松懈。对于正在快速部署 AI Agent 的企业而言，这是一记重要警示。

- **EN**：New research reveals a counterintuitive finding: organizations that express the highest confidence in their AI security posture are also the most likely to have experienced breaches.
