### HF 论文：法律 AI 幻觉率高达 52%，新框架按错误类型精准审计

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.18021
- **TitleEN**：HF Paper: Legal AI Hallucinates 52% of the Time — New Typed Auditing Framework

LegalHalluLens 论文揭示法律 AI 的幻觉率高达约 52%，但更关键的是：不同类型错误（数字/时间/义务/事实）的分布差异极大，用平均值衡量会严重误导。对于做法律 AI 产品的开发者，这个框架可以帮你找到「哪类错误最多」，从而有针对性地修复，而不是盲目调参。

- **EN**：LegalHalluLens reveals that legal AI systems hallucinate ~52% of the time on average, but this hides critical variance across error types—numeric, temporal, obligation, and factual claims fail at very different rates. For developers building legal AI products, this typed auditing framework provides actionable signals for targeted improvement rather than blind fine-tuning.

### HF 论文：ContextRL 让 LLM 真正「看进去」长上下文，推理准确率显著提升

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.17053
- **TitleEN**：HF Paper: ContextRL Teaches LLMs to Extract Critical Evidence from Long Contexts

ContextRL 提出一种上下文感知强化学习方法：不只奖励最终答案正确，还训练模型精准定位「长文档中那一行决定性证据」。对做 RAG、Agent 工具调用链或多模态推理的开发者而言，这意味着模型不再靠运气找关键信息，长上下文推理能力将有实质提升。

- **EN**：ContextRL introduces context-aware reinforcement learning that trains LLMs to pinpoint decisive evidence within long documents or tool traces, not just produce correct final answers. Developers building RAG pipelines, agentic tool-calling, or multimodal reasoning systems will benefit as models become more reliable at extracting the needle from the haystack.
