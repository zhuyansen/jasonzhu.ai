### Cursor 拟融资 $2B+，估值飙至 $500 亿

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/sources-cursor-in-talks-to-raise-2b-at-50b-valuation-as-enterprise-growth-surges/
- **TitleEN**：Cursor in Talks to Raise $2B+ at $50B Valuation

Cursor 正与 a16z、Thrive 洽谈新一轮融资，金额超 20 亿美元，估值达 500 亿美元。核心驱动力是企业客户爆发式增长。这意味着 AI 编程工具赛道正从「极客玩具」全面进入企业级市场，估值规模已逼近传统 SaaS 巨头。

- **EN**：Cursor is in talks with a16z and Thrive to raise more than $2 billion at a $50 billion valuation, driven by explosive growth in enterprise customers. AI coding tools are moving from geek toys to full enterprise adoption, with valuations now approaching those of traditional SaaS giants.

### OpenAI 关闭 Sora 并解散科学团队，两位高管离职

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/kevin-weil-and-bill-peebles-exit-openai-as-company-continues-to-shed-side-quests/
- **TitleEN**：OpenAI Shuts Down Sora and Science Team as Two Executives Depart

CPO Kevin Weil 与研究副总裁 Bill Peebles 相继离开 OpenAI，同期公司关闭 Sora 项目并裁撤科学团队。这标志着 OpenAI 正从「消费级科技大梦」向「企业 AI 基础设施」大幅转向，战略收缩信号明确。

- **EN**：CPO Kevin Weil and VP of Research Bill Peebles have both left OpenAI. At the same time, the company shut down the Sora project and disbanded its science team. These moves mark a sharp pivot from consumer tech moonshots toward enterprise AI infrastructure and send a clear signal of strategic retrenchment.

### 「Tokenmaxxing」正在让开发者的生产力打折——代码更多，成本更高，返工更频繁

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/tokenmaxxing-is-making-developers-less-productive-than-they-think/
- **TitleEN**：'Tokenmaxxing' Is Undercutting Developer Productivity: More Code, Higher Costs, More Rework

研究指出，开发者倾向于用 AI 生成大量代码（Tokenmaxxing），但实际带来的是更高的 API 成本与更多的返工负担，真实生产力提升被高估。这对用 AI 工具构建产品的独立开发者是一个重要的方法论警示。

- **EN**：Research finds that developers tend to generate large volumes of AI code, a practice dubbed 'tokenmaxxing.' The result is higher API costs and heavier rework, so real productivity gains are overstated. This is an important methodological warning for indie developers building products with AI tools.
