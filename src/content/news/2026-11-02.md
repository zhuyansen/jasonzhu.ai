### ABF-Bench让区块链Agent可验证追踪资金

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.23656
- **TitleEN**：ABF-Bench Tests Verifiable Blockchain Forensics Agents

ABF-Bench提出面向区块链取证Agent的交互式评测，要求模型在洗钱、跨链和混币等复杂场景中追踪资金并给出可核验证据。对开发者而言，它提供了比普通问答更接近真实调查流程的测试方法。

- **EN**：ABF-Bench introduces an interactive benchmark for blockchain-forensics agents. Models must trace funds through laundering, bridging, swapping, and mixing scenarios while producing independently verifiable evidence. For developers, it offers a more realistic way to evaluate tool-using agents than conventional question-answer tests.

### JEPAs证明多模态模型可能共享世界表征

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.39302
- **TitleEN**：JEPAs Provide a Theoretical Basis for Shared Representations

论文将“不同模态模型最终学到相似世界表示”的猜想推进为定理：独立训练的JEPA模型可能在旋转变换意义下收敛到同一表示。对开发者的意义是，多模态对齐或许能更多依赖共享表征，而非大量配对数据。

- **EN**：This work turns the Platonic Representation Hypothesis into a theorem for JEPA-style models: separately trained models across modalities can learn equivalent representations up to a rotation. For developers, the result suggests that multimodal alignment may rely more on shared latent structure and less on massive paired datasets.

### NBER研究生成式AI消费需求与竞争格局

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：Hacker News
- **链接**：https://www.nber.org/papers/w35953
- **TitleEN**：NBER Studies Competition in Generative AI Demand

NBER新研究从消费者需求角度分析生成式AI市场竞争，关注用户如何在不同产品之间选择，以及模型能力、价格和产品定位如何影响市场份额。对开发者而言，竞争重点不只在模型性能，也在真实使用场景与需求匹配。

- **EN**：A new NBER study examines competition in generative AI through consumer demand, focusing on how users choose among products and how capabilities, pricing, and positioning affect market share. The practical implication is that winning AI products require more than model quality: they must match concrete user needs and usage contexts.
