### 多语言推理能力终于破局：数据混合策略让模型用母语思考

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.10445
- **TitleEN**：Data Mixing Strategy Enables LLMs to Reason Natively in Non-English Languages

当前推理模型（包括 o1 类）几乎无论用户用哪种语言提问都默认用英文推理，这对非英语用户是隐形损失。这篇论文提出「数据混合」训练策略，让模型真正用目标语言推理，而非先翻译再思考。对出海 AI 产品做本地化推理能力有直接参考价值。

- **EN**：Current reasoning models default to English internal reasoning regardless of input language—a silent quality loss for non-English users. This paper's data mixing strategy trains models to reason natively in the target language. Directly actionable for developers building localized AI products for non-English markets.
