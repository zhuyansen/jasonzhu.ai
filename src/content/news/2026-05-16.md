### OpenAI 推出 ChatGPT 个人金融：直接连接银行账户

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts
- **TitleEN**：ChatGPT Launches Personal Finance with Bank Integration

OpenAI 正式发布 ChatGPT for personal finance，用户可授权 ChatGPT 直接连接银行账户进行预算分析、交易归类、消费建议。这是 ChatGPT 首次进入消费级金融场景，对国内外个人理财 SaaS（Mint / Copilot Money / Cleo 等）形成直接威胁，AI 助手正在抢占信任成本最高的家庭财务场景。

- **EN**：OpenAI has released ChatGPT for personal finance, letting users authorize direct bank account connections for budget analysis, transaction categorization, and spending advice. This marks ChatGPT's first move into consumer finance, posing a direct threat to personal finance SaaS players like Mint, Copilot Money, and Cleo as AI assistants compete for the highest-trust household money management use case.

### Anthropic 改 Claude Code 计费模式：programmatic usage 单独计量

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：Latent Space
- **链接**：https://www.latent.space/p/codex-claude-meters
- **TitleEN**：Anthropic Shifts Claude Code to Metered Programmatic Billing

Latent Space AINews 拆解 Anthropic 最新动作：Claude Code 的 programmatic（脚本/自动化调用）使用将单独计量收费，告别此前的 finger-based（按使用人头）定价。对独立开发者和小团队而言，**重度自动化用户成本可能显著上升**，需要重新评估 Agent workflow 的总拥有成本。

- **EN**：Anthropic is breaking out programmatic and automation-based Claude Code usage into separate metered billing, moving away from per-seat pricing. For independent developers and small teams running heavy automated workflows, total cost of ownership could rise sharply, forcing a reassessment of agent pipeline economics.

### bindureddy：AI 单位推理成本已正式超过雇人

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：X/@bindureddy
- **链接**：https://x.com/bindureddy
- **TitleEN**：bindureddy: AI Inference Costs Now Exceed Human Labor in Some Tasks

bindureddy 抛出反常识观察：在很多重度推理场景下，AI 单次任务成本已超过雇人完成同样任务的边际成本。半开玩笑说「Humans are sooo back! 😅」——背后真问题是：第一波「AI 比人便宜」红利在某些场景已经结束，下一轮拼的是「同等成本下质量是否真比人强」。

- **EN**：bindureddy surfaced a counterintuitive observation: in many heavy-inference scenarios, the per-task cost of AI already exceeds the marginal cost of hiring a human for the same work, joking 'Humans are sooo back!' The deeper issue is that the first wave of 'AI is cheaper than people' gains has ended in certain domains, and the next round must prove quality justifies equal or higher cost.
