### Hydra-0：用「像素动作流」统一世界模型与机器人控制

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.18077
- **TitleEN**：Hydra-0: Unified World Modeling and Robot Control via Action Flow

Hydra-0 将机器人动作表示为像素运动流，以此作为统一的视觉接口，让同一个世界模型能跨不同机器人形态、任务和环境泛化。机器人运动误差降低 90.4%，物体运动误差降低 60.2%，还支持零样本组合。对 AI 机器人开发者而言，这是「一个模型控制所有机器人」方向的重要进展。

- **EN**：Hydra-0 represents robot actions as pixel motion flows, creating a shared visual interface for a single world model to generalize across robot types, tasks, and environments. Achieves 90.4% lower robot-motion error, with zero-shot composition support—a significant step toward one model controlling all robots.

### ParaTempo：用时序置信度动态剪枝并行推理，大幅降低 CoT 计算成本

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.16425
- **TitleEN**：ParaTempo: Temporal Confidence Cuts Parallel Reasoning Compute Cost

并行推理（多路 CoT）能提升准确性但计算爆炸。ParaTempo 通过「时序置信度」动态控制每条推理分支的深度和宽度，比依赖最终答案一致性或局部 token 置信度的老方法更精准。对需要部署大推理模型的开发者来说，这是降低推理成本的实用新思路。

- **EN**：Parallel chain-of-thought reasoning boosts accuracy but explodes compute. ParaTempo uses temporal confidence signals to dynamically prune reasoning branches—more precise than final-answer consensus or token-level confidence. Practical cost-reduction technique for teams deploying large reasoning models at scale.
