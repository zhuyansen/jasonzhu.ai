### AI 对齐检测新方法：单次调用同时评估多个对齐标准

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29429
- **TitleEN**：Jev: RL-Trained Model Detects AI Alignment Failures Zero-Shot

论文提出 Jev，一个用强化学习训练的对齐失败检测模型，核心创新是「一次调用、同时输出多个对齐维度的校准概率」。对开发者的意义：部署 AI 应用时可用更低成本做多维安全审计，而不必为每条标准单独跑一个 judge 模型。

- **EN**：Jev is an RL-trained model that detects AI alignment failures zero-shot, answering multiple alignment criteria in a single forward pass with calibrated probabilities. For developers deploying AI apps, this means cheaper, faster multi-dimensional safety auditing without running separate judge models per criterion.

### 机器人双臂操作新架构 DeltaWAM：只预测「变化量」大幅提速

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.28811
- **TitleEN**：DeltaWAM: Robot World Models That Only Predict What Changes

DeltaWAM 论文提出只建模帧间「变化量」而非完整未来帧，解决了现有机器人世界模型反复重建静态背景、推理瓶颈严重的痛点。对 AI 应用开发者的意义：这类「增量推理」思路同样可迁移到视频理解、实时 Agent 决策等场景，大幅降低计算开销。

- **EN**：DeltaWAM proposes predicting only inter-frame deltas rather than full future frames for bimanual robot control, eliminating redundant computation on static content. The delta-prediction paradigm has broader implications for video understanding and real-time agent decision-making where inference speed is critical.
