### CLI-Anything把几乎所有软件变成智能体工具

- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.20461
- **TitleEN**：CLI-Anything Makes Software Agent-Native

semCER重新设计OCR错误评估方式，不再把普通拼写错误和错写人名视为同等严重，而是按错误对读者的实际影响加权。对开发者而言，这能让OCR和视觉语言模型评测更贴近真实业务损失。

- **EN**：semCER makes OCR evaluation severity-aware by distinguishing harmless typos from damaging errors such as incorrect names. Instead of treating every character edit equally, it weights mistakes by their practical impact on readers, helping developers choose models based on business-relevant quality rather than a single crude error count.
