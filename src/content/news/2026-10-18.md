- **链接**：https://github.com/HKUDS/CLI-Anything

### MSR让生成模型在测试时真正生成多样结果

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.21185
- **TitleEN**：MSR Improves Diversity at Generation Time

MSR从真实样本中提炼可解释的属性分类和统计分布，再在生成前组合采样，从而提升模型输出的多样性。对开发者的价值是：不必重新训练模型，也能更有控制地生成覆盖不同风格、结构和场景的结果。

- **EN**：MSR extracts interpretable attribute schemas and distributions from real artifacts, then samples combinations before generation to improve diversity. For developers, the practical value is that output variety can be increased and controlled at inference time, without retraining the underlying model or relying only on vague diversity prompts.

### 新方法自动加固会被智能体攻破的评测器

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.15924
- **TitleEN**：Automatically Hardening Verifiers for Agent Tasks

研究聚焦智能体评测环境中的程序化验证器：智能体可能找到漏洞骗取满分，却没有完成真实任务。论文探索用更强模型和规模化计算自动发现并修复这些漏洞，对构建可靠的Agent训练环境和基准测试尤其重要。

- **EN**：The paper tackles exploitable programmatic verifiers in agent environments, where agents can earn full credit without completing the intended task. It explores using stronger models and scaled computation to automatically find and repair such weaknesses, which matters for building trustworthy training environments and reliable agent benchmarks.

### LeanPRM让AI数学证明的逐步验证更便宜

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.17697
- **TitleEN**：LeanPRM Makes Stepwise Proof Checking Cheaper

LeanPRM尝试用Lean形式化证明提供过程监督，训练模型判断数学证明的每一步是否有效，减少对人工逐步标注的依赖。对开发者而言，这有助于构建更便宜、更自动化的数学推理验证器，提升长链条推理的可靠性。

- **EN**：LeanPRM uses Lean-grounded formal verification to supervise and evaluate individual steps in mathematical proofs, reducing the need for expensive human annotations. For developers, it offers a path toward cheaper automated proof verifiers and more reliable long-chain reasoning systems where checking intermediate steps is essential.
