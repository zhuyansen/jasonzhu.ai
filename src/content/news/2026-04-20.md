### Qwen 3.6 发布：3B 激活参数达到 Opus 4.7 八成性能

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：X/@bindureddy
- **链接**：https://nitter.net/bindureddy/status/2045393596824838361#m
- **TitleEN**：Qwen 3.6 Launches: 3B Active Parameters Reach 80% of Opus 4.7

Qwen 3.6 正式发布，仅用 30 亿激活参数即可达到 Claude Opus 4.7 约 80% 的性能水平，且运行成本极低。这标志着开源模型在性价比上对顶级闭源模型形成实质性压力，开源生态正在以惊人速度追赶头部玩家。

- **EN**：Qwen 3.6 is officially out, reaching roughly 80% of Claude Opus 4.7's performance with just 3 billion active parameters and very low running costs. It marks real price-performance pressure from open-source models on top closed-source ones, as the open ecosystem closes the gap with leading players at a startling pace.
