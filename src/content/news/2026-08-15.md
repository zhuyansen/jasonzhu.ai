This signals Anthropic is taking the agent-skills ecosystem from community experiments toward a first-party standard.

### 低频不可闻音频可绕过大型语音模型安全防护：新型红队攻击方法 ILL

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.09158
- **TitleEN**：Inaudible Low-Frequency Audio Can Bypass Speech AI Safety Guards

研究发现大型音频语言模型（LALM）存在低频安全盲区：人耳听不见的低频信号仍可进入模型并影响其输出，研究者提出 ILL（间歇低频封锁）攻击方法验证了这一风险。对开发者意义：凡是在语音产品中部署 AI 的团队，都需警惕音频输入层的新型攻击面，现有安全测试框架可能完全忽略了这一维度。

- **EN**：Researchers found large audio-language models have a critical blind spot: inaudible low-frequency signals humans can't hear can still enter the model and manipulate outputs. The new ILL red-teaming method quantifies this risk. For devs: any voice AI product needs to add audio-layer security testing — existing safety pipelines likely miss this attack surface entirely.
