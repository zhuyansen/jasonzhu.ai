### HF 论文：LedgerAgent 用「结构化状态账本」让客服 AI Agent 严格遵守业务规则

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.20529
- **TitleEN**：LedgerAgent: Structured State Ledger Makes Tool-Calling Agents Policy-Adherent

传统 Agent 把任务状态、工具返回值、策略指令全塞进 Prompt，每轮对话都要从头重建上下文，容易「忘规则」。LedgerAgent 提出把关键状态单独维护成结构化账本，Agent 只读账本不读全文。对构建企业级客服或合规 AI 的开发者来说，这是解决「Agent 违规操作」顽疾的新思路。

- **EN**：LedgerAgent separates task state into a structured ledger instead of dumping everything into the prompt. This makes tool-calling agents far more reliable at following business rules—critical for developers building enterprise customer-service or compliance AI.

### HF 论文：FID「彩票效应」揭示图像生成评测结果高度依赖随机种子，单一数字不可信

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.20536
- **TitleEN**：The FID Lottery: Single Benchmark Numbers for Image Generation Are Dangerously Misleading

FID（Fréchet Inception Distance）是图像生成模型最主流的评分标准，但研究发现该指标对训练随机种子和采样种子极度敏感，「同一模型」换个种子分数可以大幅波动。这意味着大量论文中的 SOTA 声明可能存在选择性汇报，对正在评估生成模型的开发者是重要提醒。

- **EN**：This paper treats FID as a random variable and finds its variance across training/sampling seeds is surprisingly high—meaning many published 'SOTA' image generation results may be cherry-picked. A must-read warning for anyone benchmarking or comparing generative models.
