### GeoStack：让多领域 VLM 专家模型自由拼接、不再互相「遗忘」

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.06477
- **TitleEN**：GeoStack Lets Domain-Expert VLMs Combine Without Catastrophic Forgetting

视觉语言模型（VLM）合并多个领域能力时往往发生「灾难性遗忘」。GeoStack 通过对 Adapter 流形施加几何约束，让独立训练的领域专家模块可以直接组合进统一模型，且基础能力不受损。对开发者意味着：可以像插件一样按需叠加垂直领域能力，无需从头重新训练大模型。

- **EN**：Vision-language models often suffer catastrophic forgetting when they merge capabilities from multiple domains. GeoStack applies geometric constraints to the adapter manifold, so independently trained domain-expert modules can be combined into one unified model without degrading its base abilities. For developers, this means vertical capabilities can be stacked like plugins, with no need to retrain from scratch.

- **TitleEN**：Wispr Flow's Bet on Hinglish Voice Input Accelerates India Growth
