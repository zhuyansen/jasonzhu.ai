### LLM「线性叠加假说」：Transformer 可同时持有两个独立思路

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29845
- **TitleEN**：Superposition Linearity Hypothesis: Transformers Can Hold Two Thoughts Simultaneously

论文发现 LLM 存在「线性叠加」特性：将两段不同文本流线性组合输入后，模型输出恰好是两个独立概率分布的叠加。这不是训练涌现的，而是 Transformer 架构的内在特性。对开发者意义：可解释性、多任务并行推理、以及 prompt 组合设计都将因此获得新的理论依据。

- **EN**：New paper reveals that when two text streams are linearly combined as input, LLMs output a superposition of both individual next-token distributions — an intrinsic architectural property, not a training artifact. Key implications for interpretability, parallel reasoning, and prompt composition design.
