### Fine-Tuning 为何加剧幻觉？持续学习工具或可解决这一顽疾

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.15574
- **TitleEN**：Why Fine-Tuning Worsens Hallucinations, and How Continual Learning Helps

研究发现：用 SFT 对模型做微调时，新知识的注入会破坏预训练中习得的旧知识，从而引发更多幻觉。论文借鉴「持续学习」领域的技术加以缓解。对做垂直领域微调的开发者很关键——你的模型可能越调越「编」，这篇论文给出了应对思路。

- **EN**：Research shows that when models are fine-tuned with SFT, injecting new knowledge disrupts knowledge learned during pretraining, which leads to more hallucinations. The paper mitigates this with techniques borrowed from continual learning. This matters for anyone fine-tuning domain-specific models, since more tuning may make a model fabricate more. The paper offers a way to counter that.
