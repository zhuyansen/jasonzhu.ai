### 论文：弱监督下 LLM 推理能力何时能被强化学习激活？

- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.18574

- **EN**：This paper systematically tests whether RLVR can still improve model reasoning under three weak-supervision conditions: scarce data, noisy reward signals, and self-supervision.
