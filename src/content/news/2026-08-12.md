### ChatGPT 正式开始投放广告，免费用户将看到清晰标注的商业内容

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：OpenAI Blog
- **链接**：https://openai.com/index/testing-ads-in-chatgpt
- **TitleEN**：OpenAI Starts Testing Ads in ChatGPT for Free Users

OpenAI 宣布在 ChatGPT 中测试广告功能，以维持免费访问体验。广告将清晰标注、不影响答案生成，并提供隐私保护和用户控制选项。这是 OpenAI 商业化路径的重大转折——此前一直依赖订阅收入，此举意味着平台变现逻辑正向广告生态延伸。

- **EN**：OpenAI begins testing ads in ChatGPT to sustain free access, with clear labeling, answer independence, and strong privacy controls. This marks a major business model shift for OpenAI — previously subscription-only — signaling a move toward ad-supported AI, similar to how Google monetized search.

### Omega-S：三行代码解决 LLM 微调「灾难性遗忘」难题

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.03887
- **TitleEN**：Omega-S: A 3-Line Fix for Catastrophic Forgetting in LLM Fine-Tuning

微调 LLM 时最头疼的问题之一：新数据训进去，旧能力忘光光。Omega-S 提出只需插入训练循环三行代码的惩罚项，无需历史数据、Fisher 矩阵或旧权重备份，在 Llama-3-8B LoRA 实验中显著保留了原有代码能力，计算开销仅增加 4%。对所有在自己数据上微调模型的开发者来说极具实用价值。

- **EN**：Omega-S solves LLM catastrophic forgetting with just 3 lines added to any training loop — no previous-task data, Fisher matrix, or old weight copies needed. On Llama-3-8B LoRA fine-tuning, it retains original capabilities while adding under 4% compute overhead. Extremely practical for any developer fine-tuning models on custom data.

### Business Arena：首个让 AI Agent 跑完整跨境电商业务的基准测试

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.08621
- **TitleEN**：Business Arena: New Benchmark Tests LLM Agents Running Real Businesses

Business Arena 是首个要求 AI Agent 在受控环境中完整运营跨境电商业务的基准——包括在信息不完整情况下发现机会、在不确定性下调配资金、适应延迟反馈和满足监管要求。对开发者的意义在于：现有 Agent 在「真实业务场景」下的能力边界终于有了量化标尺。

- **EN**：Business Arena is the first benchmark where LLM agents must actually run a cross-border e-commerce business end-to-end — inferring opportunities from partial data, allocating capital under uncertainty, and meeting regulatory requirements. Developers finally have a realistic yardstick for how capable (or not) today's frontier agents are at real business tasks.
