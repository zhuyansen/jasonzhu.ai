### Graphify把代码库变知识图谱，支持多家编码智能体

- **板块**：Skills 生态
- **来源**：GitHub Trending
- **链接**：https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify
- **TitleEN**：Graphify Turns Codebases into Queryable Knowledge Graphs

Graphify提供/graphify技能，可将代码、文档、SQL schema、配置文件和PDF转成可查询的知识图谱。它支持Claude Code、Cursor、Codex和Gemini CLI，在本地做确定性AST解析，每条关系都附带解释，且不依赖向量库。适合帮编码智能体快速理解大型陌生代码库。

- **EN**：Graphify ships a /graphify skill that turns code, docs, SQL schemas, configs and PDFs into a queryable knowledge graph. It works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Gemini CLI. Parsing is local and deterministic via AST, every edge is explained, and no vector store is needed. It helps coding agents understand large, unfamiliar codebases.

### OpenAI用GPT-5.6 Sol挖出影响七成网站的PHP漏洞

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：Latent Space
- **链接**：https://latent.space/p/ainews-openais-patch-the-planet-program/
- **TitleEN**：OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Finds Core PHP Flaw Affecting 70% of Web

OpenAI的Patch the Planet项目借助GPT-5.6 Sol，在PHP核心中发现PHP2Shell漏洞，影响约70%的网站。OpenAI是发现该漏洞的两方之一，相关协调修复持续了两个月。这是AI大规模挖掘基础设施级漏洞的标志性案例，运行PHP服务的开发者应尽快检查版本并更新。

- **EN**：Using GPT-5.6 Sol, OpenAI's Patch the Planet program discovered PHP2Shell vulnerabilities in core PHP, affecting roughly 70% of the web. Developers running PHP should check their versions and update promptly.

### Agent Mosaic：智能体会拼凑信息泄露机密结论

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30418
- **TitleEN**：Agent Mosaic: Agents Leak Secrets by Assembling Fragments

研究发现，智能体会从多份看似无害的文档中拼出敏感结论并说出来，这被称为“马赛克效应”。团队用网络安全、医疗、军事等合成案例测试，覆盖受控模拟中的3个模型，以及两个开源Harness中的真实智能体，共产生上万条回答。即使是善意提问，也会出现泄露。基于内部知识库做智能体的开发者，不能只靠文档级权限控制。

- **EN**：Agents can piece together a sensitive conclusion from individually harmless documents and disclose it, a risk the paper calls the mosaic effect. Tests used synthetic cybersecurity, medical and military cases across three models in simulation and live agents in two open-source harnesses, producing over 10,000 answers. Leaks occurred even under benign queries. Developers building agents over internal data cannot rely on document-level permissions alone.

### 扩散语言模型速度因任务而异，改写快创作慢

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25835
- **TitleEN**：Diffusion LLM Speed Varies Widely Across Tasks

扩散语言模型一次前向计算能生成多个token，并在生成过程中自行决定计算多少轮。因此，长度相同的两段输出，成本可能差别很大。论文在10个任务上分析了两个扩散模型，发现复制或轻改输入的内容很便宜，开放式写作则昂贵得多。开发者可据此判断哪些场景适合用扩散模型，并更准确地估算推理成本。

- **EN**：Diffusion language models can generate several tokens per forward pass and decide during generation how many passes to spend. So two outputs of the same length can differ greatly in cost. Analyzing two models across 10 tasks, the paper finds copied or lightly edited text is cheap while open-ended writing is costly. This helps developers choose suitable workloads for diffusion models and estimate inference costs more accurately.
