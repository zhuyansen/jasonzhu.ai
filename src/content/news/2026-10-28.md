### 新研究重构智能体Harness的验证与复核机制

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.34577
- **TitleEN**：New Research Rethinks Verification in Agent Harnesses

研究将智能体验证视为一种测试时扩展能力，系统分析何时介入、由谁检查以及如何推动修订，并覆盖指令、执行、结果和反馈等层级。对工程团队而言，可靠智能体的关键不只是生成答案，还包括设计可持续的复核闭环。

- **EN**：This study treats verification as a form of test-time scaling for agents, examining when to intervene, who should check the work, and how checking should trigger revision across the agent lifecycle. The practical lesson is that reliable agents require structured review loops, not just stronger generation models.
