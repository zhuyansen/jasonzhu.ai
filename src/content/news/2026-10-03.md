### 一个微型LoRA，让Transformer摆脱「浅层思考」魔咒

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.36585
- **TitleEN**：A Tiny LoRA Fixes Transformers' Shallow-Reasoning Problem

研究发现预训练Transformer实际只用极少层数跟踪上下文引用（仅1.4-3.6行），导致长链推理掉链子；仅用一个8维LoRA微调单层，就让Qwen3-8B准确率从15.5%飙升到99%，为低成本提升模型推理深度提供新思路。

- **EN**：Researchers found pretrained transformers only use 1.4-3.6 layers to track context references, causing long-chain reasoning to fail. A single rank-8 LoRA at one early layer boosted Qwen3-8B's accuracy from 15.5% to 99% on 24-line reasoning chains — a cheap trick to unlock deeper reasoning without retraining.
