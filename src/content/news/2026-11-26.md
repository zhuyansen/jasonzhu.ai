### MiniMind开源：3元成本两小时训练64M大模型

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：GitHub Trending
- **链接**：https://github.com/jingyaogong/minimind
- **TitleEN**：MiniMind Open Source: Train a 64M LLM in 2 Hours

该项目展示了如何用极低成本（3元）和极快速度在个人GPU上训练出一个64M参数的微型模型，并完整复现了从预训练、SFT到RLHF的链路。这对于研究模型极简架构和轻量化部署具有极高的教学意义。

- **EN**：This project demonstrates how to train a 64M parameter LLM from scratch on a consumer GPU with minimal cost (3 RMB) and time (2 hours). It covers the entire pipeline including pretraining, SFT, and RLHF, offering high educational value for lightweight model research and deployment.

### FNS架构：通过复杂二次动力学实现恒定存储的权重生成

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32393
- **TitleEN**：Fractal Neural Synthesis: Constant-Storage Weight Derivation via Complex Quadratic Dynamics

研究提出了分形神经合成（FNS），利用仅24字节的坐标种子替代传统庞大的权重张量。这对于在微控制器（MCU）或受限边缘设备上部署复杂模型具有革命性意义，解决了存储瓶颈。

- **EN**：The paper introduces Fractal Neural Synthesis (FNS), replacing massive weight tensors with a constant 24-byte coordinate seed. This is revolutionary for deploying complex AI models on microcontrollers or resource-constrained edge devices by solving the persistent memory footprint bottleneck.
