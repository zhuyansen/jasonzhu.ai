### Andrew Ng 开源 OpenWorker：不聊天、直接交付成品的本地 AI 同事

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：X/@AndrewYNg
- **链接**：https://x.com/AndrewYNg
- **TitleEN**：Andrew Ng Launches OpenWorker: Open-Source AI Agent That Delivers Finished Work

Andrew Ng 发布开源 AI Agent 项目 OpenWorker，主打「不聊天只干活」——直接产出润色好的文档、发 Slack 消息、更新日历。本地运行、自带 API key、支持 GPT、Claude Fable、Gemini、Kimi、DeepSeek 等多模型，数据不离机。面向重视隐私和模型自主权的开发者。

- **EN**：Andrew Ng open-sourced OpenWorker, an AI agent that skips the chat and delivers finished outputs — polished docs, Slack messages, calendar updates. Runs locally on Mac, supports multiple LLMs (Claude Fable, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Ollama), with all data staying on your machine unless you choose otherwise.

### DocOps：专为 Agent 操作复杂文档设计的可验证基准

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.19865
- **TitleEN**：DocOps: A Verifiable Benchmark for AI Agents in Complex Document Operations

DocOps 提出了一套可确定性验证的 Agent 文档操作评估框架，将现实工作场景中的文档操作分解为原子维度和递进复杂度。对开发者的意义：如果你在构建能处理 Word/PDF/表格的 AI Agent，这是目前最贴近真实工作流的测试基准，能清晰暴露模型在哪个步骤掉链子。

- **EN**：DocOps introduces a deterministically verifiable benchmark for AI agents performing complex document operations, decomposing real-world workflows into atomic steps and escalating complexity levels. For developers building document-handling agents, this is the most realistic evaluation framework yet — showing exactly where models fail in practice.

### 超网络注入知识的 Scaling Law：LoRA 适配器让模型精准记住新事实

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.19604
- **TitleEN**：Scaling Laws for Hypernetwork Knowledge Injection Into LLMs via LoRA Adapters

研究发现，训练一个超网络（Hypernetwork）来生成 LoRA 适配器，可以在训练阶段高效向 LLM 注入大规模事实知识，并且遵循清晰的 Scaling Law。对开发者的意义：这为「无需重训整个大模型就能让它学会新知识」提供了可预测、可量化的工程路径，对知识库类 AI 产品很有价值。

- **EN**：Researchers found that training a hypernetwork to generate LoRA adapters can inject large-scale factual knowledge into LLMs at training time, following predictable scaling laws. For developers: this offers a quantifiable, cost-efficient path to updating model knowledge without full retraining — highly relevant for knowledge-base AI products.
