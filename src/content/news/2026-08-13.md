### InSight-doc：AI 自适应分辨率「阅读」超长文档的新框架

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.10628
- **TitleEN**：InSight-doc: Agentic Visual Perception That Selectively Zooms Into Long Documents

InSight-doc 提出一种 Agentic 视觉感知框架：先以低分辨率浏览长文档，再自动放大关键区域精读，无需外部检索器。对开发者的意义在于：处理 PDF 报告、财报、合同等多页文档时，推理成本将显著降低，精度更高。

- **EN**：InSight-doc is an agentic framework for long documents—starts low-res, selectively zooms into important regions without an external retriever. For developers: this means cheaper, more accurate AI parsing of multi-page PDFs, contracts, and financial reports.
