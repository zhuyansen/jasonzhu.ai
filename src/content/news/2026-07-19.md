### 小米发布机器人大模型：10万小时真实轨迹训练VLA基础模型

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.15330
- **TitleEN**：Xiaomi Robotics-1: VLA Model Trained on 100K+ Hours of Real Trajectories

小米发布Xiaomi-Robotics-1，一个视觉-语言-动作（VLA）基础模型，基于超10万小时真实世界操作轨迹训练。模型开箱即用可执行多样化移动操作任务，且仅需少量微调数据即可快速迁移到新任务。这是迄今训练数据规模最大的机器人通用模型之一，意味着具身智能离真正商用化又近了一步。

- **EN**：Xiaomi releases Xiaomi-Robotics-1, a vision-language-action model trained on over 100K hours of real-world robot trajectories. It handles diverse manipulation tasks out-of-the-box and adapts to new tasks with minimal fine-tuning data — one of the largest-scale embodied AI models to date, signaling serious progress toward commercial robotics.

### RAGU开源GraphRAG引擎：两阶段知识图谱抽取大幅降低噪声

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.11683
- **TitleEN**：RAGU: Open-Source GraphRAG Engine Cuts Knowledge Graph Noise with Two-Stage Extraction

RAGU是一款开源模块化GraphRAG引擎，解决了现有系统一次性抽取导致知识图谱噪声多、检索脆弱的问题。它将抽取与整合分离，通过两阶段类型化抽取、DBSCAN去重、LLM摘要和社区检测来构建更干净的知识图谱。对于需要构建企业知识库RAG系统的开发者而言，这是一个可以直接上手的高质量开源方案。

- **EN**：RAGU is an open-source GraphRAG engine that separates extraction from consolidation, using two-stage typed extraction, DBSCAN deduplication, LLM summarization, and Leiden community detection. It produces cleaner knowledge graphs than single-pass systems — a ready-to-use solution for developers building enterprise RAG pipelines.

### VideoRAE：让视频生成模型理解「语义」，不只是像素重建

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.14088
- **TitleEN**：VideoRAE: Teaching Video Generation Models to Encode Semantics, Not Just Pixels

VideoRAE提出用「表示自编码器」将V-JEPA 2、VideoMAEv2等视频基础模型的强语义理解能力，直接注入到视频生成模型的潜在空间。传统3D-VAE只优化像素重建，导致时空语义结构弱。VideoRAE弥补了这一缺口，对需要高质量视频生成（如Sora类产品）的开发者有直接参考价值。

- **EN**：VideoRAE injects the semantic understanding of video foundation models (V-JEPA 2, VideoMAEv2) into video generation latent spaces via representation autoencoders. Conventional 3D-VAEs optimize for pixel reconstruction and miss spatio-temporal semantics — VideoRAE bridges this gap, directly relevant to developers building high-quality video generation systems.

### DSWorld：数据科学Agent的「世界模型」，预测执行结果减少试错

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.15901
- **TitleEN**：DSWorld: A World Model for Data Science Agents to Predict Before Executing

DSWorld提出「数据科学世界模型」概念：让自主数据科学Agent在真正执行操作前，先预测工作流的状态变化。现有Agent严重依赖昂贵的试错循环，DSWorld通过建模执行环境的状态转移来大幅降低计算成本。对构建数据分析自动化Agent的开发者而言，这个思路可以显著提升效率。

- **EN**：DSWorld introduces a 'Data Science World Model' concept that lets autonomous agents predict the outcome of data operations before actually running them, bypassing expensive trial-and-error loops. For developers building automated data analysis agents, this approach could dramatically cut compute costs and iteration time.

### xHC：突破Transformer残差流瓶颈的新型超连接扩展架构

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.14530
- **TitleEN**：xHC: Expanded Hyper-Connections That Scale Transformer Residual Streams Beyond N=4

xHC（扩展超连接）解决了现有Hyper-Connections方法在N>4后性能边际递减甚至下滑的问题，提出了新的稳定化方案。简单说：这是一种让Transformer「记忆带宽」在宽度和深度之外继续扩展的新轴向，对模型架构研究者而言是一个值得关注的潜在新缩放维度。

- **EN**：xHC solves the performance degradation problem in Hyper-Connections (HC) when scaling beyond N=4, introducing a new stabilization method. In plain terms: it's a new scaling axis for Transformers beyond width and depth — a potentially important architectural insight for anyone working on next-generation model design.
