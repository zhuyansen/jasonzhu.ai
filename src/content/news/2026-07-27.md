### Dataset Distillation 新方法 Inf-Match：用「参数影响」对齐替代梯度对齐

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.16859
- **TitleEN**：Inf-Match: Outcome-Centric Dataset Distillation via Influence Matching

传统数据集蒸馏对齐的是训练过程中的梯度轨迹，而 Inf-Match 换了思路：直接对齐「训练结束后参数的变化量」。通过可微分的样本级影响力估计器，学出一个紧凑合成数据集，其对模型权重的影响力与完整数据集等效。对于需要用小数据集高效微调模型的开发者，这个方法有潜力大幅降低数据准备成本。

- **EN**：Instead of matching gradient trajectories during training, Influence Matching (Inf-Match) aligns the final outcome: a compact synthetic dataset whose effect on converged model parameters mirrors the full dataset. For developers who need to fine-tune efficiently with minimal data, this could dramatically cut data preparation costs.
