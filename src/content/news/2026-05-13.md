### InfoLaw 信息缩放定律：教你在数据有限时如何配比训练数据才不亏

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.02364
- **TitleEN**：InfoLaw: Scaling Laws for Optimal Data Mixing Under Limited Data

论文提出 InfoLaw 框架，解决了一个实际痛点：高质量数据上调权重训练时，数据量不足会导致重复率升高、性能反而下降。InfoLaw 能在不同数据配比和重复训练条件下准确预测损失，帮助开发者在有限数据预算下找到最优训练配方，对微调和预训练实践直接适用。

- **EN**：A new paper proposes InfoLaw to tackle a practical problem. When you upweight high-quality data but don't have enough of it, repetition rises and performance drops. InfoLaw accurately predicts loss across different data mixtures and repetition levels. This helps developers find the best training recipe on a limited data budget, and it applies directly to both fine-tuning and pretraining.
