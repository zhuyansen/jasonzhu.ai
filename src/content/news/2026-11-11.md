### RidgeFill跳过部分提示词计算以加快首字响应

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.09709
- **TitleEN**：RidgeFill Targets Faster Prompt Processing Without Model Retraining

RidgeFill用一次拟合的映射预测模型后部层状态，减少读取提示词时的计算，无需重新训练模型权重。对自建推理服务的开发者，它提供了缩短首字等待的新思路，但加速收益与精度影响仍需实测。

- **EN**：RidgeFill reduces prompt-processing work by predicting inputs to later model layers and using those predictions to populate their caches. It fits mappings once rather than retraining the model's weights. For developers operating inference infrastructure, this offers a potential route to lower time to first token, with accuracy and speed trade-offs requiring workload-specific validation.

### e-means尝试以更少训练轮次改进向量聚类

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.09501
- **TitleEN**：E-means Aims to Improve Clustering With Fewer Epochs

e-means以小批量更新和指数移动平均改进k-means，论文称可用约十分之一轮次取得更好表现，并给出参数选择规律。对开发者，其意义是有望降低向量分组、数据整理及检索预处理成本。

- **EN**：The e-means paper introduces mini-batch updates with exponential moving averages for k-means, claiming better results in roughly one-tenth the epochs. It also provides guidance linking batch size and decay settings to performance. Developers working on embedding clustering, retrieval preparation, quantization, or data curation may gain a more efficient preprocessing option, subject to validation on their datasets.
