### ARCS用结构化澄清减少Text-to-SQL误解

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.09396
- **TitleEN**：ARCS Reduces Text-to-SQL Errors Through Disambiguation

ARCS提出结构化消歧方法，专门处理用户自然语言提问中隐含的口径和业务语义歧义。相比单纯让用户反复澄清，它尝试把不确定意图拆解并校正，开发者可借此提升数据分析Agent生成SQL的准确率与可控性。

- **EN**：ARCS introduces structured disambiguation for text-to-SQL systems, targeting subtle domain-specific ambiguities that can silently produce incorrect queries. Instead of relying entirely on repeated user clarification, it structures and resolves uncertain intent. For developers, this offers a practical direction for making analytics agents more accurate and safer in real business databases.

### 研究发现GUI Agent经验并非处处有用

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.33304
- **TitleEN**：GUI Agent Memory Helps Early Decisions Most

研究发现，GUI Agent检索历史经验能明显改善任务开始时的第一步决策，但对后续每一步并不总有帮助，相关经验甚至可能造成干扰。对开发者而言，关键不是无限扩大记忆，而是根据当前决策阶段精准激活经验。

- **EN**：This study finds that retrieved interaction history strongly improves the opening decision of a personal GUI agent, but its value often declines for later steps and may even introduce distractions. The result suggests that agent memory should be activated selectively by decision stage, rather than indiscriminately appended to every context window.
