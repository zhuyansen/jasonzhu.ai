### Constitutional Midtraining：在预训练阶段植入价值观，对齐更持久

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.26654
- **TitleEN**：Constitutional Midtraining Makes AI Alignment More Durable

Anthropic 研究发现，后训练对齐（RLHF）很浅，微调一下就会褪色。新方法「宪法中间训练」在预训练与后训练之间插入一个专门包含价值观内容的训练阶段（394M token 语料基于 Anthropic 宪法），在 120B 规模验证后对齐效果更稳定、更难被破坏。对需要可信部署的企业场景极为重要。

- **EN**：Anthropic's constitutional midtraining inserts a values-based training stage between pre- and post-training at 120B scale. Unlike shallow RLHF alignment that erodes under fine-tuning, this approach produces durable alignment — critical for enterprise deployments where model robustness under adaptation matters.

### 不是所有 Token 都值得同等奖励：反事实敏感度重新分配长链推理信用

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.27888
- **TitleEN**：Not All Tokens Equal: Counterfactual Credit Reallocation Boosts Long-CoT Reasoning

当前主流 RLVR 方法（如 GRPO）把整条响应的奖励平均分给每个 token，完全忽略不同 token 对最终结果的贡献差异。新研究提出反事实敏感度信用重分配方法，精准识别「哪些 token 真正影响了推理结果」并差异化分配奖励，显著提升长链推理（Long-CoT）训练效果。对做 RLVR 微调的开发者有直接参考价值。

- **EN**：Current RLVR methods like GRPO uniformly spread reward across all tokens — ignoring that some tokens matter far more to the outcome. This paper proposes counterfactual sensitivity credit reallocation to identify and reward high-impact tokens, meaningfully improving long-CoT reasoning training for developers doing RLVR fine-tuning.
