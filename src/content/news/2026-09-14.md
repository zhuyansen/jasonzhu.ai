### 神经符号系统的致命漏洞：形式化翻译「看起来对」但语义全错

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.11085
- **TitleEN**：Neurosymbolic AI Has a Hidden Bug: Correct Verdicts, Wrong Semantics

研究者发现神经符号系统存在「判决保留式不忠」（VPU）漏洞：AI 对数学问题的形式化翻译能通过验证器检查，但实际语义与原题不同，也就是说「答对了但理解错了」。这对依赖 LLM 做数学推理验证的开发者是重要预警——现有验证机制存在系统性盲区。

- **EN**：Researchers identify a critical flaw called Verdict-Preserving-Unfaithfulness (VPU) in neurosymbolic AI: a model's formal translation of a math problem can pass solver verification while being semantically wrong. A critical warning for developers relying on LLMs for formal reasoning or math verification pipelines.
