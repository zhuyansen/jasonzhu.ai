### 线性注意力架构综述：DeltaNet系列如何在长上下文中替代Transformer

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.07953
- **TitleEN**：Linear Attention Survey: How DeltaNet Variants Challenge Transformers at Long Context

这篇论文系统对比了标准 Softmax 注意力与四种线性注意力架构（DeltaNet、Gated DeltaNet、Kimi Delta Attention、Gated DeltaNet-2）。对开发者的直接意义：传统 Transformer 在长上下文下计算成本呈二次方增长，线性注意力可以突破这一瓶颈——这直接影响未来长文档处理、代码库分析等 Agent 任务的可行性与成本。

- **EN**：This paper benchmarks standard softmax attention against four linear-attention architectures including Kimi Delta Attention and Gated DeltaNet-2. For developers: as Transformer costs scale quadratically with context length, linear attention variants offer a credible path to affordable long-context processing for Agent tasks like codebase analysis and document reasoning.

### DrugGen 2：结合疾病本体的AI小分子药物设计模型

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.08404
- **TitleEN**：DrugGen 2: Disease-Aware Language Model for AI-Driven Drug Discovery

DrugGen-2 在 GPT-2 基础上微调，同时以疾病本体和目标蛋白序列为条件生成小分子药物候选物，首次将疾病上下文纳入分子生成流程。对 AI+生物医药开发者的意义：这类「条件生成」范式可大幅缩短早期药物发现中的候选分子筛选周期，降低进入临床前的试错成本。

- **EN**：DrugGen-2 fine-tunes GPT-2 to generate drug candidates conditioned on both disease ontology and target protein sequences — the first model to explicitly incorporate disease context. For AI/biotech developers, this conditioned generation approach could dramatically accelerate early-stage drug discovery and reduce costly trial-and-error before preclinical testing.
