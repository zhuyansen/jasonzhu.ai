### 新论文：轻量决策模型替代LLM，边缘推理大幅提速

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.22753
- **TitleEN**：Jev Decision Models Cut LLM Latency at the Edge

研究者提出用轻量「Jev决策模型」替代大语言模型处理边缘服务请求的意图判断，只提取4-8个关键字段再调度执行，大幅砍掉等待LLM决策消耗的延迟预算——对需要毫秒级响应的IoT、客服路由类AI应用很实用。

- **EN**：Researchers swap LLMs for lightweight 'Jev decision models' in edge service orchestration, extracting just 4-8 bounded intent fields instead of running a full language model. This slashes the latency budget LLM decisions eat up—practical for IoT or real-time routing where milliseconds matter.

### 新论文Persona Dosing：像调音量一样精准控制AI人格

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.36388
- **TitleEN**：Persona Dosing Lets You Dial AI Personality Precisely

这篇论文提出「人格剂量」概念：不只是开关某种性格特质，而是像调音量一样精确控制AI表达该特质的强度等级，且无需为每个强度单独标注训练数据。对需要可控分级人格定制的陪伴AI、客服Agent有直接应用价值。

- **EN**：This paper introduces 'persona dosing'—precisely dialing an AI's personality trait intensity like a volume knob, rather than just switching traits on/off, without needing intensity-labeled training data. Directly useful for companion AI or support agents needing fine-grained personality control.

### Honeycomb：给视频世界模型装上「恒定大小」场景记忆

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.37690
- **TitleEN**：Honeycomb Gives Video World Models Fixed-Size Memory

针对长时视频生成中场景记忆随时长线性膨胀的问题，Honeycomb用六个空间/时空平面构成固定大小的低秩记忆（HexMemory），让AI生成长视频时既能记住场景细节又不会内存爆炸——对游戏、机器人仿真的世界模型落地很关键。

- **EN**：Long video generation typically needs ever-growing memory to stay consistent. Honeycomb fixes this with HexMemory, a constant-size low-rank memory using six spatial/spatiotemporal planes—letting AI remember scene details over long videos without memory blowing up, key for gaming and robotics simulation.
