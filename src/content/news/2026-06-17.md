### HF 论文：时序差异驱动视觉表征学习，无需人工标注也不靠强归纳假设

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.15956
- **TitleEN**：Visual Representation Learning via Temporal Differences Drops Strong Inductive Biases

新研究提出用视频帧间「时序差异」作为自监督信号学习视觉表征，无需人工标注、无需强归纳假设。对开发者的意义：这条路线可能让视觉模型预训练更通用、更省标注成本，尤其适合数据稀缺的垂直场景（医疗、工业等）自建视觉基础模型。

- **EN**：This paper proposes using temporal differences between video frames as a self-supervised signal for visual representation learning — no human labels, no strong architectural assumptions needed. Developers building vision models for data-scarce verticals (medical, industrial) could benefit from cheaper, more generalizable pretraining pipelines.

### HF 论文：探索性 RL 让 LLM 自主发现推理原语，无需人工指定训练方向

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.17024
- **TitleEN**：ExpRL: LLMs Self-Discover Reasoning Primitives via Exploratory Reinforcement Learning

ExpRL 提出在 LLM 中训练阶段引入探索性强化学习，让模型自主发现分解、验证、自我纠错等推理原语，而无需人工手动标注「该学什么」。对开发者的意义：这可能大幅降低构建高质量推理模型的数据工程成本，是 Post-Training 流程的重要简化方向。

- **EN**：ExpRL introduces exploratory RL during LLM mid-training, letting models autonomously discover reasoning primitives like decomposition and self-correction — without manually curated training traces. This could dramatically cut data engineering costs for building strong reasoning models, a notable simplification of post-training pipelines.
