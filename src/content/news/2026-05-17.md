### ArXiv 出手：让 AI 全程代写论文将被封禁一年

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/research-repository-arxiv-will-ban-authors-for-a-year-if-they-let-ai-do-all-the-work/
- **TitleEN**：ArXiv Bans Papers Fully Written by AI

全球最大预印本平台 ArXiv 宣布新规：如果作者让 AI 完成论文的全部工作，将被封禁投稿资格一年。这是顶级学术基础设施第一次以制度形式给「AI 代写」划红线——AI 辅助写作可以，AI 全权代劳不行。对所有用 AI 做学术产出的人来说，「人类实质贡献」从道德建议变成了硬规则。

- **EN**：ArXiv, the world's largest preprint platform, announced that authors who delegate an entire paper to AI face a one-year submission ban. This marks the first time a major academic infrastructure has institutionally drawn a hard line on AI ghostwriting. AI assistance remains permitted, but full AI authorship does not. 'Meaningful human contribution' has shifted from ethical suggestion to enforceable rule.

### Curl 维护者公开宣布：AI 安全报告不再是垃圾

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：Hacker News
- **链接**：https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/04/22/high-quality-chaos/
- **TitleEN**：Curl Maintainer: AI Security Reports No Longer Garbage

Curl 项目维护者 Daniel Stenberg 曾长期吐槽 AI 生成的安全漏洞报告是「slop（垃圾）」，浪费维护者时间。他最新博客态度转变：AI 安全报告质量已显著提升，开始出现真正有价值的发现。这是 AI 在安全研究领域从「噪音制造者」转向「有效贡献者」的标志性拐点。

- **EN**：Curl maintainer Daniel Stenberg, who previously dismissed AI-generated vulnerability reports as 'slop,' has reversed his stance in a new blog post. He notes that report quality has improved significantly and genuinely valuable findings are now emerging. This marks a turning point for AI in security research — moving from noise generator to credible contributor.

### Agent Swarms：让顶级 LLM 自己造 Agent 的多智能体系统

- **板块**：Skills 生态
- **来源**：X/@bindureddy
- **链接**：https://x.com/bindureddy
- **TitleEN**：Agent Swarms: Top LLMs Dynamically Generating New Agents

bindureddy 介绍 Agent Swarms 概念——一个多 Agent 系统，能调用 Opus 4.7 等顶级模型「自己生成新的 Agent」来完成子任务。这是 Agent 架构从「人写死 Agent」走向「Agent 动态生成 Agent」的演进方向，对构建复杂自动化工作流的开发者是值得关注的范式。

- **EN**：Bindureddy introduces Agent Swarms — a multi-agent system that uses top-tier models like Opus 4.7 to dynamically generate new agents for subtasks on the fly. This represents an architectural evolution from hardcoded agents to self-spawning agent networks, and is a noteworthy paradigm shift for developers building complex automated workflows.
