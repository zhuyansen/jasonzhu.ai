### 论文：知识图谱让 LLM Agent 真正记住你是谁

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2506.17001
- **TitleEN**：Paper: Knowledge Graphs Help LLM Agents Truly Remember Users

PersonalAI 论文系统比较了多种基于知识图谱的个性化记忆方案。对开发者的意义：RAG 只能检索事实，但知识图谱能结构化存储用户偏好和历史关系，是构建真正「记得住用户」的 Agent 产品的关键技术路径。

- **EN**：The PersonalAI paper systematically compares several knowledge-graph-based approaches to personalized memory. For developers, the takeaway is that RAG can only retrieve facts, while knowledge graphs store user preferences and historical relationships in structured form. That makes them a key technical path to building agent products that genuinely remember their users.
