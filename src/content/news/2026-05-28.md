- **链接**：https://www.wired.com/story/illinois-pass-major-ai-safety-law-pritzker/

- **EN**：The Illinois legislature passed what is being called the toughest AI safety bill in the country, covering frontier model disclosure, synthetic content labeling, and AI decision-making transparency. With state regulation outpacing federal action, teams building AI products in the US must now navigate a patchwork of state-level compliance obligations, significantly raising legal and compliance budgets.
