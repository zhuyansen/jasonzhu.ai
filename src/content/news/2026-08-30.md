### Luce：AI生成3D资产终于支持物理真实感重新打光

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.23943
- **TitleEN**：Luce: AI-generated 3D assets with physically accurate relighting

Luce提出了一种新型3D表示方法，将几何形状与PBR材质（漫反射、金属度、粗糙度、法线）统一在一套体素化高斯云中。实际意义：图生3D的结果终于可以直接导入Blender/Unreal等标准渲染管线并自由换光源，而非只能在固定光照下展示——这是游戏、影视、电商场景的关键缺口。

- **EN**：Luce introduces a new 3D representation that unifies geometry and PBR materials (albedo, metallic-roughness, normals) in a single voxelized Gaussian cloud. For developers: image-to-3D outputs can now be dropped directly into standard render pipelines like Blender or Unreal and relit freely — finally closing a critical gap for games, film, and e-commerce use cases.
