### Cloudflare 向 AI 公司发最后通牒：9 月前须区分爬虫用途或遭封锁

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/cloudflares-new-policy-pushes-ai-companies-to-pay-for-publishers-content/
- **TitleEN**：Cloudflare Gives AI Companies Until Sept 15 to Separate Crawlers or Face Blocks

Cloudflare 宣布新政策：AI 公司必须在 9 月 15 日前将「搜索爬虫」与「AI 训练/Agent 爬虫」分开标识，否则将在大量出版商网站上被默认封锁。这是内容方向 AI 公司收费的重要一步，将深刻影响 RAG 和 Agent 的数据获取链路。

- **EN**：Cloudflare is giving AI companies a September 15 deadline to separate search crawlers from AI training/agent crawlers. Failure to comply risks being blocked by default across publisher sites — a major shift in how AI companies can access web content for training and agents.

### TRIAGE：给 Agent 强化学习中每个动作精准分配信用的新框架

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.32017
- **TitleEN**：TRIAGE: Role-Typed Credit Assignment Fixes a Core Problem in Agentic RL

现有 Agent 强化学习（如 GRPO）用最终结果统一奖励所有动作，导致「有用的探索被惩罚，无效动作被强化」。TRIAGE 为每个动作步骤按「角色类型」分配差异化信用，大幅提升 Agent 训练效率。对于构建搜索、点击、编辑等多步 Agent 的开发者，这个框架能让训练出的模型更精准、更少走弯路。

- **EN**：Standard agentic RL treats all actions equally using final outcome as reward — punishing useful exploration in failed runs. TRIAGE assigns role-typed credit to each action step, making agent training significantly more precise. Practical for developers building multi-step agents that search, click, or edit.
