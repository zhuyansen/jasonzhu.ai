### 「思维链让模型更安全」是误解：HF 新研究发现 Thinking Tokens 并不改善对齐

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.25013
- **TitleEN**：Thinking tokens don't reliably improve AI safety, new HuggingFace research finds

普遍认为「让模型先思考再回答」能提升安全性，但这项覆盖 GPT-OSS、Qwen、OLMo、Phi 等主流推理模型的研究发现：Thinking Tokens 并不总能阻止有害输出，在某些情况下甚至更容易被越狱。对开发者的警示：别把 CoT 当安全护栏，需要单独的对齐机制。

- **EN**：Widely assumed that 'think before answering' improves safety, but this multi-model study (GPT-OSS, Qwen, OLMo, Phi) shows thinking tokens don't reliably prevent harmful outputs and can sometimes make models easier to jailbreak. For developers: don't treat chain-of-thought as a safety layer — it isn't one.

### Agent 上下文管理是「承重墙」：计划最先被压缩，也最致命

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.22953
- **TitleEN**：Context compression kills agent plans first — a new diagnostic reveals the hidden cost

研究揭示：长程 Agent 在上下文压缩时，「计划」是最早被丢弃的信息，而计划丢失会导致后续步骤隐性失效。论文提出「replay pairing」诊断法，通过比较有无计划的轨迹隐层状态差异来量化损失。对构建 Multi-step Agent 的开发者：上下文管理策略必须优先保护「计划节点」。

- **EN**：Long-horizon agents compress context by evicting early tokens — and plans are evicted first. This paper introduces 'replay pairing' to measure how much plan loss degrades downstream steps. Key takeaway for agent developers: your context management strategy must explicitly protect planning tokens, or your agent silently loses its goals.
