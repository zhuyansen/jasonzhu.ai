### Shadcn发布improve：强模型规划，便宜模型执行

- **板块**：Skills 生态
- **来源**：GitHub Trending
- **链接**：https://github.com/shadcn/improve
- **TitleEN**：Shadcn Launches Improve for Agentic Code Planning

Shadcn推出improve Agent Skill，先让能力最强的模型审计代码库、判断改动价值并生成实施计划，再交给成本更低的模型执行。它把高价值的理解与规划从具体编码中拆分出来，降低Agent开发成本。

- **EN**：Shadcn’s improve skill audits a codebase with a capable model, decides what is worth changing, and produces implementation plans for cheaper models to execute. The approach separates high-value reasoning from routine coding, potentially reducing agent costs while preserving planning quality.

### ScrapeBench揭示AI爬虫合规率低于5%

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.11306
- **TitleEN**：ScrapeBench Finds Most AI Agents Fail Scraping Compliance

ScrapeBench首次系统测试AI Agent是否遵守网站服务条款和robots.txt，发现多数闭源与开源Agent的合规率低于5%。这意味着自动化采集上线前，开发者必须增加规则解析、权限判断、审计和人工兜底。

- **EN**：ScrapeBench evaluates whether AI agents follow website terms of service and robots.txt directives during scraping. Most tested open- and closed-weight agents achieved compliance rates below 5%. Developers building browser or research agents should add policy parsing, authorization checks, logging, and human fallback before deployment.

### 新推理基础设施让LLM服务随Agent负载进化

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12304
- **TitleEN**：Evolving Infra Adapts LLM Serving to Agent Workloads

Evolving Infra提出面向Agent工作负载的自适应LLM服务框架，动态调整Prefill与Decode资源，并更好管理可复用的KV缓存。它针对Agent请求波动和多轮复用问题，有望减少负载失衡、缓存驱逐和推理浪费。

- **EN**：Evolving Infra introduces an adaptive serving framework for agentic workloads. It dynamically adjusts compute between prefill and decode while managing reusable KV caches more effectively. The design targets bursty, multi-turn agent traffic and could reduce load imbalance, cache eviction, and wasted inference capacity.

### 不确定时才升精度：小模型推理节省权重带宽

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14418
- **TitleEN**：Adaptive Precision Cuts Weight Bandwidth for Local Models

Precision on Demand让本地模型默认用低精度权重解码，仅在输出不确定时重新用高精度计算。在Qwen3-0.6B和GSM8K测试中，这种按Token动态升精度的方法有望在保持答案质量的同时降低内存读取和推理成本。

- **EN**：Precision on Demand decodes with low-precision weights by default and retries uncertain tokens at higher precision. Tested on Qwen3-0.6B with GSM8K, the method suggests local models can reduce weight-memory traffic while selectively preserving accuracy where the model is least confident.
