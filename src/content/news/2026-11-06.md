### D3D让扩散草稿模型异步加速大模型解码

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06425
- **TitleEN**：D3D Enables Asynchronous Speculative Decoding

D3D提出独立的扩散式草稿模型，在目标模型验证期间继续生成候选Token，去除传统推测解码的轮次依赖。对开发者而言，这意味着在不改变目标模型的情况下，推理服务有机会获得更高吞吐和更低延迟。

- **EN**：D3D introduces a standalone diffusion drafter that can keep producing candidate tokens without waiting for updated hidden states from the target model. For developers, this could improve speculative decoding throughput and latency while preserving the existing target model, making acceleration easier to integrate into inference systems.

### Multi-layer Indexer优化DeepSeek稀疏注意力

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.03768
- **TitleEN**：Multi-layer Indexer Speeds Sparse Attention

论文分析DeepSeek稀疏注意力中的索引瓶颈，并提出Multi-layer Indexer，让多层注意力选择过程重叠执行。它的价值在于减少长上下文推理中扫描KV缓存的额外开销，帮助开发者进一步压低显存占用和服务成本。

- **EN**：This work targets the indexing bottleneck in DeepSeek-style sparse attention and proposes overlapping selection across layers. Its practical importance is reducing the extra cost of scanning the KV cache during long-context inference, potentially lowering memory use and serving costs for developers deploying sparse-attention models.
