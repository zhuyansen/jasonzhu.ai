### 研究发现长上下文真正难点是信息竞争

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.04169
- **TitleEN**：Context Competition Matters More Than Length

研究发现，模型处理长上下文时，真正造成检索能力下降的不是文本长度，而是相互竞争的事实：加入一个与答案冲突的陈述，准确率可能下降9至110倍。开发者应减少冲突信息，而非只盯上下文窗口大小。

- **EN**：A study finds that retrieval failures in long contexts are driven less by raw length than by competing facts. On several open models, a single rival statement can multiply accuracy loss by 9 to 110 times. Developers should manage contradictory context, not just expand the context window.

### 视觉攻击可让机器人改动作却不改解释

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.04166
- **TitleEN**：Visual Attacks Can Hide Changes in Robot Actions

研究显示，针对视觉输入的扰动可以改变驾驶或机械操作模型的实际动作，却让模型生成的安全解释保持逐字不变。对开发者而言，语言理由不能单独作为具身Agent安全性的验证依据。

- **EN**：The paper shows that visual perturbations can alter a vision-language-action model’s physical behavior while leaving its natural-language rationale unchanged word for word. For developers, this means verbal explanations alone cannot reliably validate the safety of embodied agents.

### Uber数据显示AI Agent叫车几乎无人使用

- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/almost-no-one-uses-ai-agents-to-book-their-uber-company-says/
- **TitleEN**：Uber Says Almost Nobody Books Rides via Agents
