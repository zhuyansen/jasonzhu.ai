### HF 论文：多智能体 LLM 系统存在四类并发异常，已用 TLA+ 形式化验证

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.17182
- **TitleEN**：Multi-Agent LLM Concurrency Bugs Formally Verified in TLA+

研究者发现多智能体 LLM 系统在共享内存、向量索引和工具注册表时，存在「过时生成」「幽灵工具」「因果级联」「工具效果乱序」四类并发 Bug，并用 TLA+ 形式化证明了每类异常的存在。**对开发者的意义**：你的 Multi-Agent 系统可能正在悄悄产生错误结果——这篇论文给出了诊断清单和防御设计模式。

- **EN**：Researchers formally identified four concurrency bugs in multi-agent LLM systems — stale-generation, phantom-tool, causal-cascade, and tool-effect reordering — verified with TLA+ proofs. Developers building multi-agent pipelines now have a concrete checklist of race conditions to guard against in production.

### HF 论文：LLM「遗忘」其实只是表面——微调几步就能把知识找回来

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.17168
- **TitleEN**：LLM Unlearning Is Shallow: Fine-Tuning Easily Recovers 'Forgotten' Knowledge

RepSelect 论文揭示了 LLM「遗忘」（Unlearning）领域的核心漏洞：现有方法只是浅层压制，只需少量微调或 few-shot 提示就能把「遗忘」的内容找回来。**对开发者的意义**：依赖遗忘技术做合规或版权保护的产品，安全假设可能从根本上就是错的——这个问题比想象中严重得多。

- **EN**：RepSelect shows that current LLM unlearning methods only achieve shallow forgetting — a few fine-tuning steps or few-shot prompts can recover supposedly deleted knowledge. For developers and compliance teams relying on unlearning for data removal or copyright protection, the security assumptions may be fundamentally broken.
