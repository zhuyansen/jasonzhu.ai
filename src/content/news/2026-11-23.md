### 扩散模型RL微调本质拆解：激活引导贡献显著

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26627
- **TitleEN**：RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Acts as Activation Steering

强化学习后训练能显著提升扩散模型的画质、文字渲染与构图能力。这篇论文检验了其中有多少增益来自与输入无关的激活偏移，帮助开发者看清 RL 微调机制，未来有望用更轻量的激活引导替代昂贵训练。

- **EN**：RL post-training improves visual quality, text rendering, and composition in diffusion transformers. This paper probes how much of that gain stems from simple input-independent activation shifts, offering developers insights into lighter alignment methods.

### VLA具身模型安全急停研究：填补中途拦截漏洞

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26239
- **TitleEN**：Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Stop Safely Mid-Task

研究发现，能在初始状态拒绝违规指令的机器人 VLA 模型，一旦在任务执行中途接管仍可能继续完成危险动作。论文探讨了中途急停与物理响应控制，对开发者提升具身智能的现实安全性至关重要。

- **EN**：Policies that refuse prohibited tasks at the start can still finish them if taking over mid-execution. This paper investigates safe mid-task stopping and physical response control, crucial for developers building reliable real-world VLA robotics.

### 自动驾驶策略感知数据估值：按真实收益筛选数据

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25604
- **TitleEN**：Policy-Aware Data Valuation for End-to-End Autonomous Driving

端到端自动驾驶并非数据越多越好，对某个规划器有益的样本可能损害另一个。该研究提出策略感知数据估值方法（PAD），直接按对下游策略的实际收益筛选数据，帮助开发者避免盲目堆料、提升训练性价比。

- **EN**：Scaling autonomous driving demonstrations does not guarantee better policies, as data aiding one planner may hurt another. PAD evaluates samples by downstream policy utility rather than heuristics, helping developers curate higher-impact training sets.

### 时序因果发现新算法ITPD，应对高维与分布偏移

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26232
- **TitleEN**：Local Causal Parent Discovery for Complex Time Series Data

时间序列中的自相关、分布偏移和高维灾难常让传统因果发现算法算力爆炸。论文提出迭代时间父节点发现算法 ITPD，在保证完备性的同时降低计算与样本复杂度，为开发者处理复杂动态时序建模提供新工具。

- **EN**：Autocorrelation, distribution shifts, and high dimensionality hinder causal discovery in time series. This work introduces Iterative Temporal Parent Discovery (ITPD), reducing computational and sample complexity for practical temporal modeling.
