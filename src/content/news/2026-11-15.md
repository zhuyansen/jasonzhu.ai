### 黄仁勋离任冲击AI创业融资，VC预期支票缩水

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：TechCrunch AI
- **链接**：https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/jensen-huangs-departure-blasts-big-hole-in-ai-startup-funding-vcs-and-founders-say/
- **TitleEN**：Jensen Huang's Exit Leaves Hole in AI Startup Funding

黄仁勋在执掌英伟达33年后辞任CEO，Colette Kress出任临时CEO。英伟达此前向AI创业公司投入数百亿美元，曾是2026年最活跃的投资方之一。多位创始人和VC表示，正为更小的支票和更低的估值做准备。AI创业者需要重新评估融资节奏，并分散对单一战略投资方的依赖。

- **EN**：Jensen Huang stepped down after 33 years leading Nvidia, with Colette Kress named interim CEO. Nvidia had poured tens of billions into AI startups and ranked among 2026's busiest investors. Founders and VCs now expect smaller checks and lower valuations, so AI startups may need to rethink fundraising timelines and reduce reliance on any single strategic backer.

### MixUA：多用户多Agent共享记忆，同时守住隐私

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.10439
- **TitleEN**：MixUA: Shared Agent Memory Across Users With Privacy Controls

让Agent跨用户共享经验能加速自我进化，但必须解决两个问题：谁能用这条经验，哪些信息必须保密。MixUA把各用户与Agent的交互轨迹提炼成经验，并按粒度分配给合适的用户或Agent。对做多租户Agent产品的开发者来说，这为共享学习与数据隔离提供了一种可行的设计思路。

- **EN**：Sharing lessons across users speeds up agent self-improvement, but it requires deciding who benefits and what stays private. MixUA distills user-agent trajectories into lessons and assigns them at the right granularity. For builders of multi-tenant agent products, it offers a workable pattern for combining shared learning with data isolation.

### PixVid把潜空间视频模型升级到像素空间生成

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17336
- **TitleEN**：PixVid Lifts Latent Video Diffusion Into Pixel Space

主流视频生成模型在VAE压缩的潜空间中工作，带来几个限制：分辨率翻倍会让token数变为四倍，控制条件只能作用于帧组，响应还要等待整个帧块生成完。PixVid能把已预训练的潜空间模型直接迁移到像素空间，有望让高分辨率、逐帧可控、实时交互的视频生成更容易实现。

- **EN**：Frontier video models run in a compressed VAE latent space: doubling resolution quadruples tokens, conditions apply only to frame groups, and responses wait on whole frame blocks. PixVid lifts pretrained latent models directly into pixel space, potentially making high-resolution, per-frame controllable and interactive video generation easier for developers.
