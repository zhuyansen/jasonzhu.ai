### OpenAI 联合博通发布 Jalapeño 推理芯片，专为 LLM 推理优化

- **板块**：AI 工具动态
- **来源**：OpenAI Blog
- **链接**：https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip
- **TitleEN**：OpenAI & Broadcom Launch Jalapeño, Custom LLM Inference Chip

OpenAI 与博通联合推出自研 AI 推理芯片 Jalapeño，专为大语言模型推理场景设计，目标是提升性能、降低推理成本并支撑更大规模的 AI 系统部署。这是 OpenAI 首次公开自研硅片级硬件，标志着其从「模型公司」向「算力垂直整合」迈出关键一步，对 Nvidia 的推理市场构成潜在竞争压力。

- **EN**：OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño, a custom inference chip purpose-built for LLM workloads. This marks OpenAI's first public foray into custom silicon, aiming to cut inference costs and reduce reliance on Nvidia—a significant vertical integration move that could reshape the AI compute landscape.

### AGORA 基准：测试 AI Agent 在真实杂乱职场文档中的推理能力

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.24526
- **TitleEN**：AGORA Benchmark: Testing LLM Agents on Messy Real-World Workplace Documents

现有 Agent 评测多在「干净数据」上进行，AGORA 基准专门模拟真实职场场景：Agent 需在大量杂乱文件中定位稀疏证据、协调术语不一致、跨文档计算结论。对开发者的价值在于：如果你的 RAG/Agent 产品要落地企业场景，AGORA 是目前最贴近真实挑战的评测框架，能帮你早发现「实验室跑得好、实战翻车」的问题。

- **EN**：AGORA introduces a benchmark specifically testing LLM agents on messy, real-world workplace archives—sparse evidence, inconsistent terminology, multi-document reasoning. For developers building enterprise RAG or agent products, this is the most realistic stress-test available: if your agent fails AGORA, it will fail in production.

### InSight：让机器人 VLA 模型自主习得新技能，无需额外人工示范

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.24884
- **TitleEN**：InSight: Robots That Teach Themselves New Skills Without Human Demos

VLA（视觉-语言-动作）模型的能力上限受制于训练数据中的技能范围。InSight 框架通过将动作拆解为可操控的基元（如「移动夹爪到碗边」「向上抬起」），让 VLA 模型能够自动分割示范、自主探索并习得新技能。对 AI 机器人开发者意义重大：这意味着机器人可以在部署后持续自我扩展能力边界，而无需昂贵的人工数据标注。

- **EN**：InSight enables Vision-Language-Action robot models to autonomously acquire new manipulation skills beyond their training data by decomposing actions into steerable primitives. Key implication for robotics developers: robots can now self-expand capabilities post-deployment without costly new human demonstrations—a meaningful step toward self-improving robotic agents.
