### HF 论文：只需少量样本，CORE 让模型推理能力快速跃升

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.28742
- **TitleEN**：CORE Method Boosts Model Reasoning With Minimal Training Data

CORE（对比反思算法）让模型比较自己过去的推理过程，找出对错差异后快速修正——不需要 RLVR 那样的大规模训练，用极少样本就能在推理任务上显著提升。对独立开发者意义：微调成本可能大幅下降，小数据集也能训出强推理模型。

- **EN**：CORE (Contrastive Reflective algorithm) trains models to compare their own past reasoning steps, identify right-versus-wrong differences, and self-correct rapidly — without the large-scale training required by RLVR. Significant reasoning gains are achievable with very few samples, potentially slashing fine-tuning costs and enabling strong reasoning models from small datasets.

### HF 论文：IPT 让视觉语言模型真正「脑补」看不见的空间

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.03988
- **TitleEN**：IPT Gives Vision-Language Models True Spatial Imagination

研究引入「想象感知 Token（IPT）」，让 VLM 在推理时生成虚拟视角的中间表示，解决遮挡、跨视角等空间推理难题。对开发者的意义：机器人导航、AR 场景理解、3D 应用中 VLM 的空间能力有望直接提升，无需更换底座模型。

- **EN**：Researchers introduced Imagined Perception Tokens (IPT), allowing vision-language models to generate intermediate representations of virtual viewpoints during inference. This tackles occlusion and cross-angle spatial reasoning challenges. Developers building robot navigation, AR scene understanding, or 3D applications could see direct spatial capability gains without swapping out their base models.
