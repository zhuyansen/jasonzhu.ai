### 上下文匹配蒸馏：解决自回归视频生成的因果错位难题

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.13391
- **TitleEN**：Context-Matched Distillation fixes causal mismatch in autoregressive video generation

现有视频生成蒸馏方法用「双向教师」监督「因果学生」，导致训练时上下文与推理时严重错位，生成质量和可控性双双下降。这篇论文提出上下文匹配蒸馏（CMD），强制教师和学生使用相同的历史帧，对需要低延迟、实时可控视频生成的开发者而言，是落地交互式视频 Agent 的关键技术突破。

- **EN**：Existing video distillation pipelines supervise causal student models using bidirectional teachers, causing a context mismatch that degrades quality and controllability. Context-Matched Distillation (CMD) forces teacher and student to share identical historical context, a critical fix for developers building low-latency, interactively controllable video generation applications.

### 知识编辑新范式：让 LLM 无需重训即可精准更新复杂非结构化知识

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.11660
- **TitleEN**：Hybrid-Policy Self-Editing enables precise unstructured knowledge updates in LLMs without retraining

LLM 训练于静态语料，知识快速过时是企业部署的核心痛点。这篇论文提出混合策略自编辑（HPSE），支持用自由格式文本段落一次性更新多条相关事实，同时不影响模型其他知识。对需要保持知识新鲜度的企业 AI 产品开发者而言，这条路比微调更轻量、更精准。

- **EN**：Large language models trained on static data go stale fast — a key enterprise pain point. Hybrid-Policy Self-Editing (HPSE) enables updating multiple related facts at once using free-form text passages, without touching unrelated knowledge. For developers building enterprise AI products that need fresh, accurate knowledge, this is a lighter and more precise alternative to fine-tuning.
