### Ponytail压缩智能体代码，测试覆盖率反而提升

- **板块**：Skills 生态
- **来源**：GitHub Trending
- **链接**：https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail
- **TitleEN**：Ponytail Cuts Agent Code While Improving Tests

Ponytail是面向Claude Code的编码Skill，主张让智能体像资深开发者一样少写代码、先复用和验证。项目称在39项任务中减少53%代码、41%时间和26%成本，同时让高风险逻辑测试覆盖率从68%升至98%。

### ATEST让推理模型按难度和GPU负载动态花算力

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25519
- **TitleEN**：ATEST Makes Test-Time Compute Resource-Aware

ATEST针对推理时多次采样的浪费问题，根据问题难度、额外答案带来的预期收益和当前GPU负载动态分配计算预算。研究发现同样采样四次，GPU拥堵时能耗最高可增4.1倍，对部署推理模型的成本控制很有价值。

- **EN**：ATEST dynamically decides whether additional reasoning samples are worth their cost, considering query difficulty and live GPU contention. The paper reports that the same four-sample query can consume up to 4.1 times more energy under heavy load. For developers, this points toward inference systems that trade off answer quality, latency, and operating cost in real time.
