### HF 论文：AI 同行评审可被「纯文字包装」操控，无需任何隐藏指令

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.13044
- **TitleEN**：AI Peer Review Can Be Gamed With Presentation Tweaks Alone, No Hidden Prompts

研究发现：仅修改论文摘要、贡献描述、相关工作等「表达层」内容（不改方法、数据、结论），就能显著提升 AI 评审系统给出的评分。对开发者的警示：任何用 LLM 做内容质量评估的系统（论文、代码、提案审核）都可能被纯「包装优化」欺骗，需要引入对抗性测试。

- **EN**：Researchers show that AI peer review systems can be manipulated by tweaking only presentation-level text — abstract framing, related work, contribution language — with zero changes to methods or results. Critical warning for developers building LLM-based evaluation pipelines: your quality filters may be fooled by sophisticated copywriting alone.

### HF 论文：大推理模型存在「评估盲点」——能解题却看不出错误推理

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.01462
- **TitleEN**：Large Reasoning Models Excel at Solving Problems But Fail to Spot Flawed Reasoning

研究用 VAIR 数据集测试发现：大型推理模型（LRMs）擅长解题，但在「答案正确但推理过程有明显错误」的题目上，无法识别推理漏洞。这与人类「评估强于生产」的认知规律相反。对 AI Agent 开发者意味着：不能用模型自我检查来替代真正的推理验证机制。

- **EN**：Using the VAIR dataset, researchers find that large reasoning models (LRMs) fail to detect flawed reasoning when the final answer is still correct — the opposite of human cognition where evaluation outpaces production. For Agent builders: self-verification loops using the same model are fundamentally unreliable and need external validation.
