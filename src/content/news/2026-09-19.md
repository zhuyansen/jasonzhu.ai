### 自进化搜索索引：让 RAG 系统根据检索环境自动优化索引表示

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.19656
- **TitleEN**：Self-Evolving Search Index: RAG Systems That Auto-Optimize Their Own Indexes

论文提出「自进化搜索索引」框架，核心洞察是：固定的索引优化策略无法适应多样的检索环境。该框架让索引能随检索任务动态调整文档表示方式，解决了 RAG 系统中「用一套索引打天下」的根本缺陷。对构建生产级 RAG 或 Agent 检索管道的开发者来说，这是提升检索质量的关键方向。

- **EN**：This paper proposes a self-evolving search index that dynamically adapts document representations to different retrieval environments, addressing the core flaw of RAG systems: one fixed index strategy can't serve all tasks. For developers building production RAG pipelines or agent retrieval layers, this offers a principled path to meaningfully better retrieval quality.

### 推理采样策略比样本数量更重要：测试时计算扩展的隐藏变量

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.19499
- **TitleEN**：How You Sample Matters More Than How Many: Hidden Variable in Test-Time Scaling

论文发现，测试时扩展（生成多个候选答案再综合）中，「生成多少个候选」远不如「怎么生成」重要——同样 N 个候选，批量一次生成 vs 分多次顺序生成，能耗和性能差异显著。对使用 best-of-N 或多数投票推理的开发者来说，优化采样策略是免费的性能提升空间。

- **EN**：This paper reveals that in test-time scaling, the candidate-generation strategy matters far more than the raw sample count N. The same N candidates produced in one batch vs. sequential smaller batches yields dramatically different energy costs and performance — a free optimization lever for developers using best-of-N or majority-vote inference.
