### WorldDiT：无需大型预训练 VLM，机器人动作与视觉世界建模统一扩散架构

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.23909
- **TitleEN**：WorldDiT: unified diffusion transformer couples robot action with visual world modeling

WorldDiT 提出用单一扩散 Transformer 同时生成机器人连续动作序列和预测未来相机帧的 RGB 画面，完全不依赖庞大的预训练 VLM。对开发者的意义：大幅降低机器人策略训练的算力门槛，「世界模型 + 动作控制」可以在一个轻量架构里端到端训练，加速具身智能落地。

- **EN**：WorldDiT couples action generation and visual world modeling in a single diffusion transformer — no massive pretrained VLM required. Significantly lowers compute barriers for robot policy training and makes end-to-end embodied AI development more accessible.

### 大模型幻觉检测新方法：清理推理链中的「噪声步骤」来提升判断准确率

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.22098
- **TitleEN**：Reasoning Denoiser: cleaning noisy reasoning traces to improve hallucination detection

大型推理模型（LRM）生成答案前的长推理链里，充斥着「无关步骤」和「重复步骤」两类噪声，严重干扰幻觉检测。Reasoning Denoiser 提出先对推理轨迹降噪再做幻觉判断，显著提升检测准确率。对构建 AI Agent 质检流水线和 RAG 可信度验证的开发者有直接参考价值。

- **EN**：Reasoning Denoiser identifies two noise types in LRM reasoning traces — irrelevant and repetitive steps — and denoises them before hallucination detection. Direct value for developers building quality assurance pipelines for AI agents and RAG systems.
