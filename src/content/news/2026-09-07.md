### VeriPhy：AI 生成视频的物理可信度审计系统，逐帧验证力学规律

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03153
- **TitleEN**：VeriPhy: Agentic System That Audits Physical Plausibility of AI-Generated Video

VeriPhy 解决了一个被忽视的问题：AI 生成的视频「看起来流畅」不等于「物理上可信」。它用 Agent 架构把文字 prompt 编译成物理约束条件，在视频生成时逐帧验证力学规律是否被违反。对做视频生成、游戏引擎、仿真训练的开发者意义重大——你的 AI 不再只是「美观」，还能「合理」。

- **EN**：VeriPhy audits AI-generated video for physical plausibility, not just visual quality. An agentic planner compiles prompts into typed physical obligations and verifies them frame-by-frame during generation. For developers in video gen, game engines, or simulation training, this means your AI output can be both beautiful and physically coherent.

### RoboTok：从互联网视频海量挖掘机器人训练数据的引擎

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03199
- **TitleEN**：RoboTok: Internet-Scale Engine for Retrieving Human Demo Videos for Robot Training

机器人训练数据采集昂贵且覆盖面窄——RoboTok 试图打破这一瓶颈，通过学习 3D 手部轨迹的隐空间，从海量互联网视频中自动检索与目标任务相关的人类操作演示。对具身智能和机械臂开发者而言，这意味着训练数据成本可能大幅下降。

- **EN**：Robot training data is expensive and sparse — RoboTok breaks this bottleneck by learning a latent motion space from 3D hand trajectories to retrieve relevant human manipulation demos from web videos at internet scale. For robotics and embodied AI developers, this could dramatically cut the cost of acquiring task-specific training data.
