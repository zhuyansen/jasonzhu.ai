### 电脑操控 Agent 效率优化：让 AI 只在关键步骤调用大模型

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.27151
- **TitleEN**：Making Computer-Use Agents Efficient: Call Large Models Only When Needed

论文提出「步骤级优化」方案：现有 Computer-use Agent 每步都调用大模型，成本高且慢；新方法让 Agent 区分「简单步骤用小模型、复杂决策用大模型」，大幅降低推理成本。对构建 GUI 自动化产品的开发者极具参考价值。

- **EN**：A new paper proposes step-level optimization for computer-use agents. Current agents call a large model at every step, which is slow and expensive. The new approach routes simple steps to small models and saves large models for complex decisions, cutting inference costs dramatically. It's a highly relevant reference for developers building GUI automation products.

### 微调会悄悄破坏模型安全性：100 个模型的大规模实证研究

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.24902
- **TitleEN**：Fine-Tuning Quietly Erodes Model Safety: A 100-Model Empirical Study

研究分析了 100 个微调模型（含医疗、法律领域），发现基础模型的安全对齐在微调后普遍发生「漂移」——即使微调数据本身无害。对所有在生产环境部署微调模型的开发者来说，这意味着上线前必须重新评估安全性，不能沿用基础模型的测试结论。

- **EN**：An analysis of 100 fine-tuned models, including medical and legal ones, found that the safety alignment of base models commonly drifts after fine-tuning, even when the fine-tuning data is harmless. Any developer deploying fine-tuned models in production must re-evaluate safety before launch rather than rely on the base model's test results.
