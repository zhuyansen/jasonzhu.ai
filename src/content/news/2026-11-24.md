### Search-Native预训练：从底层赋予模型原生搜索能力

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26809
- **TitleEN**：Search-Native Pretraining Teaches Search During Pretraining

传统大模型仅在后训练阶段学习调用搜索。该研究在预训练语料中规模化注入网页搜索查询与本地索引结果，让模型原生掌握检索逻辑，开发者无需复杂微调即可获得更可靠的Agent工具调用底座。

- **EN**：Most models learn web search only during post-training, leaving brittle tool-use behavior. This paper proposes Search-Native Pretraining, augmenting standard pretraining corpora with search queries and locally indexed web responses. For developers, this builds stronger native retrieval habits directly into base models.

### 无标注视频自蒸馏：让多模态模型学会预测未来动作

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26954
- **TitleEN**：On-Policy Self-Distillation Trains Video Foresight Without Labels

视觉语言模型预测后续事件往往依赖昂贵人工标注或大模型蒸馏。新方法利用“过去-未来”视频片段构造自监督信号进行策略内自蒸馏，大幅降低多模态视频推理与具身智能规划的训练数据门槛。

- **EN**：Training vision-language models to anticipate future actions usually requires costly annotations or larger teacher models. This paper introduces an on-policy self-distillation method using unlabeled past-future video pairs as natural supervision, offering developers a scalable way to train video foresight for robotics and agents.

- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.19247

### NextUnit-Bench实测：智能体两小时定制建模

- **板块**：AI 论文
- **来源**：HuggingFace Papers
- **链接**：https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25252
- **TitleEN**：NextUnit-Bench Tests LLM Agents Building Custom Industrial Models

与其追求单一通用预测模型，不如让编码智能体为每个新任务现场建模。该研究在24个工业设备数据集上测试智能体两小时交付可执行模型的能力，验证了“通用建模智能体”在工程落地中的潜力。

- **EN**：Instead of building one universal predictive model, can LLM coding agents act as general modelers for each new task? NextUnit-Bench tests agents given two hours across 24 industrial datasets to deliver deployable fault-prediction models, highlighting practical agent-driven AutoML workflows.
