## Project Introduction

This open-source project is maintained by princehuang (骑龙) and is dedicated to sharing hands-on experience gained through building websites for international markets. It covers the entire workflow from registering an overseas company to accepting payments.

## Payment Setup Roadmap

1. **Register an Overseas Company** - Choose the right state to form an LLC or Corp
2. **Apply for an EIN** - Obtain a US tax identification number
3. **Get a US Business Bank Card** - Open a corporate bank account
4. **Activate Stripe** - Integrate your payment system
5. **Repatriate Funds** - Move money back to your home country

## Key Takeaways

- When registering a US company, Wyoming is a popular choice — it has no corporate or personal income tax
- You can apply for an EIN yourself or hire someone on Fiverr to handle it for you
- For banking, Mercury and similar banks are recommended for their friendliness toward international founders
- Once you have a legal entity and an EIN, activating Stripe is straightforward
