## Chapter 4: How to Create Short-Form Visual & Written Content

### Four Common Content Formats

| Format | Difficulty | Engagement | Follower Growth | Best Use Case |
|--------|-----------|------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Story | ⭐⭐⭐ | High | Strong | Personal experience sharing |
| List | ⭐⭐ | Medium-High | Strong | Tool roundups, method compilations |
| Opinion | ⭐⭐⭐ | High | Moderate | Industry insights, controversial takes |
| Tutorial | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Medium | Strong | Technical walkthroughs, how-to guides |

### Writing Tips by Format

- **Story**: Draw on real experiences to create resonance; open with conflict or a hook
- **List**: Lead with a number, keep the structure clean, and pack in high information density
- **Opinion**: Take a clear stance, back it up with reasoning, and invite discussion
- **Tutorial**: Break it into clear steps, support with screenshots, and make readers genuinely grateful

### AI Content Remixing Prompts

Use AI tools to rewrite, translate, and expand existing material — turn one piece of content into many.
