## Why Choose Wise?

Once your UK company is registered, signing up for a Wise business account is the natural next step — and it's refreshingly straightforward.

**Key advantages of Wise for Business:**

- No monthly fees
- One-time account opening fee
- Receive payments in multiple currencies worldwide
- Unlocks access to Stripe for Business, with lower transaction fees than PayPal and similar platforms

## How to Sign Up

1. Verify your identity using a Chinese mobile number
2. Enter both your UK registered address and your business address
3. Complete identity verification by following the on-screen prompts
4. Pay the one-time account opening fee

## The Wise + Stripe Combo

With a Wise business account in hand, you can apply for Stripe for Business. Compared to PayPal and similar channels, Stripe's rates are considerably lower — making this the go-to combination for accepting international payments.

## Tips

- Have your UK Certificate of Incorporation ready
- Make sure your address details match exactly what you used when registering the company
- Be prepared to provide additional identity documents during the application process

## FAQ

### How do I open a business bank account for a UK company?

Once your UK company is registered, head straight to Wise to open a business account: verify with a Chinese mobile number, enter your UK registered address and business address, complete identity verification, and pay the one-time account opening fee.

### Does Wise charge a monthly fee for business accounts?

No monthly fees — just a one-time account opening fee. It supports multi-currency payments worldwide, making it one of the lowest-cost options for accepting international payments.

### Can I use Wise together with Stripe?

Absolutely. A Wise business account lets you apply for Stripe for Business, which offers lower rates than PayPal and similar platforms. Together, they're the ultimate combo for global payment collection.
