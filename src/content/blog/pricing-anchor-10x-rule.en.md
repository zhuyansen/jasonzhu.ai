## Marketing Strategy: The 10x Rule of Price Anchoring

We charge this much because our customers get at least 10x the value in return.

Don't lead with features — lead with money and outcomes.

- ❌ For just $10, you get Feature #1, #2, #3, and #4.
- ✅ For just $10, you can avoid spending $5,000 a month on a therapist.

The core logic of pricing isn't "what is my product worth?" — it's "how much will the user save or earn by using it?" When you can demonstrate that your product delivers 10x the value of its price, the price stops being an obstacle.
