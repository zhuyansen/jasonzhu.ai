## Project Overview

This guide is maintained by Qiufeng (@qiufenghyf) and documents the full, practical process of setting up a US company and getting paid as an indie developer.

## Cost Summary

**$193 total** — US company registration + EIN + Stripe + Mercury Bank

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Register a US Company ($150, ~1 week)

Register in Wyoming — the state has no corporate or personal income tax, and anyone, regardless of nationality, can register there.

Register through [wyomingagents.com](https://www.wyomingagents.com/):
- Fill in your basic information as prompted
- Choose LLC as your company type (recommended for individuals and small teams)
- Use your actual address
- Leave the optional add-ons at their default settings
- Registration typically completes within one week

### Step 2: Apply for an EIN ($43, ~3 days)

You can apply directly through the IRS website, or hire someone to do it for you. A convenient option is to find a service provider on Fiverr — just send them your company formation documents.

### Step 3: Mercury Bank (Free, ~5 days)

Once you have your US company entity and EIN, the application is straightforward. You'll need to provide proof of address (a utility bill showing your name and address).

### Step 4: Stripe (Free, ~1 day)

With all of the above in place, you can apply for Stripe directly.

### Step 5: BOI Filing ($9)

File through wyomingagents.com. Have your passport ready.

### Step 6: Wise Account ($31 account opening fee)

Supports sending and receiving payments from virtually anywhere in the world. To open a business account, you'll need: a US company + EIN, a domestic address, a passport, and a linked US bank account.

### Step 7: Tax Filing ($50)

File Form 1120 and Form 5472 through a service provider on Fiverr.

## Free Cloud Credits for Startups

After registering your US company, you can also apply for free cloud resources from major providers:
- GitHub Enterprise — 12 months (valued at $5,040)
- Azure — up to $150,000 in credits
- AWS — $15,000 in credits
- Google Cloud — $2,000 in credits
- Nvidia cloud resources
