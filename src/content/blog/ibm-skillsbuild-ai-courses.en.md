## Stage 1 Deep Dive: Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals (~10 Hours, 6 Modules)

🔗 [skillsbuild.org AI Fundamentals](https://skillsbuild.org/adult-learners/explore-learning/artificial-intelligence)

IBM's flagship entry-level credential requires **zero coding background** and takes **roughly 10 hours** across six structured modules (IBM has also introduced a refreshed two-part AI Fundamentals track expanding into generative AI and AI agents—budget 10–20 hours if you complete both):

| Module | Core Focus |
|---|---|
| **1. AI Concepts** | Core definitions, narrow vs. broad AI, and real-world use cases |
| **2. Natural Language Processing & Computer Vision** | How AI processes text, speech, and visual data |
| **3. Machine Learning & Deep Learning** | Supervised/unsupervised learning, neural networks, and model training basics |
| **4. Watson Studio Hands-On Simulation** | Guided no-code lab building and testing an AI model |
| **5. AI Ethics & Governance** | Bias, fairness, transparency, and responsible deployment |
| **6. Your Future in AI** | Career pathways, job roles, and next-step learning plans |

**How to claim your free digital credential:** Pass the course assessments with **80% or higher**, then sign into **Credly** using the **exact same email address** you used for IBM SkillsBuild. Your verifiable IBM digital badge can be added directly to your LinkedIn Licenses & Certifications section.

## FAQ

### How long does IBM SkillsBuild Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals take, and is it really free?

Yes, it is 100% free—including all 6 modules, the hands-on Watson Studio simulation, and the official digital credential. Most learners finish **Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals** in **about 10 hours** (or 10–20 hours if you also complete IBM's refreshed two-part expansion covering generative AI and AI agents).

### What is the signup trap on IBM SkillsBuild?

IBM SkillsBuild divides users into High School Students, College Students, and **Adult Learners**. If you are 18+ and want access to the full 1,000+ course library and career paths, you must register under **Adult Learners**—picking a student profile silently hides roughly half of the AI catalog.

### How do I claim my IBM SkillsBuild Credly digital badge and add it to LinkedIn?

Pass the course assessments with a score of **80% or higher**, then log in to **Credly** with the **same email address** you used on IBM SkillsBuild. From Credly, accept the official IBM digital badge and click the one-click LinkedIn share button to publish a verifiable credential on your profile.

### Is IBM SkillsBuild worth it for career changers compared to Microsoft or Anthropic?

Yes—especially for beginners, students, and non-technical professionals transitioning into AI. IBM SkillsBuild gives you the strongest completely free Credly badge plus job-workflow courses (*AI for Customer Service, Marketing, and HR*). Once you want cloud-lab verification or proctored developer credentials, pair it with [Microsoft Applied Skills / AI-901](/en/blog/microsoft-learn-ai-certifications) or compare all five major vendors in our [2026 AI Certifications Comparison](/en/blog/ai-certifications-comparison-2026).
