IBM SkillsBuild is the **undisputed leader in free courses among major tech platforms** — **1,000+ courses**, all completely free.

But there's one **major trap** that catches a lot of people, and most of them never realize it:

> ⚠️ **Choose the wrong account type at signup and half the courses disappear.**

The platform splits users into three categories: **High School Students / College Students / Adult Learners**. You need to select **Adult Learners** to access the full AI learning catalog. Pick the wrong one and roughly half the courses are simply hidden from you.

**Main site:** [skillsbuild.org](https://skillsbuild.org/)

## The AI Learning Path Has Three Stages

### Stage 1 · Build Your Foundation

**Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals**

🔗 [skillsbuild.org AI Fundamentals](https://skillsbuild.org/adult-learners/explore-learning/artificial-intelligence)

10–20 hours to complete, and you'll earn an **official IBM digital badge**. This is where you build your core understanding of AI.

### Stage 2 · Apply AI to Real-World Scenarios

Three workflow-focused courses, **especially well-suited for working professionals looking to transition into AI**:

- **AI for Customer Service**
- **AI for Marketing**
- **AI for HR**

These aren't abstract theory — they slot AI directly into the workflows of specific job functions. Odds are, at least one of them maps closely to your current role.

### Stage 3 · Go Deep on a Career Track

Three specialized paths to choose from based on where you want to take your career:

- **AI Developer Path**
- **Data Scientist Path**
- **IT Engineer Path**

Complete any path and you'll receive an **official IBM digital badge** you can add directly to your LinkedIn profile.

## FAQ

### Is there a catch when signing up for IBM SkillsBuild?

The big one: you must select **Adult Learners** as your account type. If you register as a high school or college student, half the AI courses will be hidden from your account.

### Are IBM digital badges actually worth anything?

IBM official digital badges are verifiable and LinkedIn-ready. Your first one comes from completing **Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals** — 10–20 hours of work.

### Is IBM SkillsBuild a good fit if I'm trying to change careers while still working?

It's an excellent fit. The AI for Customer Service, Marketing, and HR courses skip the abstract theory and plug AI directly into real job workflows. If you're currently employed, at least one of these will likely match what you do today.
