Overseas business generally falls into two categories: SaaS (web or app products) and cross-border e-commerce.

- **Cross-border e-commerce** involves supply chain and physical product costs
- **SaaS** primarily involves software costs along with your own time and skill investment

## SaaS Monetization Strategies

Only about 10% of people building SaaS products or indie apps abroad actually turn a profit. Common paths to monetization include:

- Building SEO-driven websites
- Running Google Ads
- Driving traffic through UGC or AI-generated content
- Pivoting to content creation

Most people don't make money from the product itself — they find a monetization path through content and traffic. Which route makes sense depends entirely on the resources and skills you bring to the table.
