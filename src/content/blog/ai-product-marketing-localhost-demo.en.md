AI Product Marketing Blueprint

Run a localhost demo, gauge market reaction, collect waitlist signups.

You don't need a polished product. You don't need a deployed server. You don't even need a registered domain. Just spin up a demo locally, record a screencast or go live, and watch how the market actually responds. If people are willing to sign up for your waitlist, you're heading in the right direction. If nobody cares, pivot early.

This is the lowest-overhead marketing strategy for the AI era — validate your biggest assumptions at the smallest possible cost.
