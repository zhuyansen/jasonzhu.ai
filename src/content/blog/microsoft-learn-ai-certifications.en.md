**Bottom line (JasonZhu.AI 2026 guide):** Across Big Tech, **Microsoft is the most generous with AI credentials**. Every self-paced course, practice assessment, and hands-on **Applied Skills** micro-credential on Microsoft Learn is **100% free**; only proctored certification exams—such as **AI-901** (replacing retired AI-900) and **AI-103** (replacing retired AI-102)—require exam fees. Most learners only need Tiers 1–2 plus Applied Skills.

**Main portal**: [learn.microsoft.com/ai](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/ai/)

> ⚠️ **2026 Retirement Heads-Up**: Both **AI-900** and **AI-102 retired on June 30, 2026**. They have been replaced by **AI-901** (*Azure AI Fundamentals*) and **AI-103** (*Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate*). Double-check the exam code before buying prep materials or booking a seat.

## Tier 1 · Foundational (Free Self-Paced + Entry Exam)

Zero prerequisites. All self-paced Microsoft Learn modules are free—finish them first before deciding whether to pay for the proctored AI-901 exam or start stacking free Applied Skills badges.

### Introduction to AI in Azure (AI-901 Self-Paced Course)

🔗 [learn.microsoft.com AI-901 Course](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/courses/ai-901t00)

The official self-paced course for AI-901, covering core Azure AI, Microsoft Foundry, generative AI, and agent concepts. This is the updated replacement for the retired AI-900 and is the recommended starting point for beginners.

### Fundamentals of Generative AI (Single Module · One Hour)

🔗 [learn.microsoft.com Generative AI Fundamentals Module](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/fundamentals-generative-ai/)

Just one module, completable in about an hour. A fast way to get up to speed on the fundamentals of generative AI — ideal if you're short on time.

## Tier 2 · Role-Based Paths (Choose by Job Function)

Pick the path that matches your role. This is the tier where most people should actually invest their time.

🔗 [learn.microsoft.com/credentials/browse](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/browse/)

- **Business roles** → **AI Business Professional**: No coding required — great for marketing, operations, and management
- **Engineering roles** → **Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate (AI-103, formerly AI-102)**: The core developer certification focused on building generative AI apps and agentic solutions with Python and Microsoft Foundry (AI-102 retired on June 30, 2026)
- **Data roles** → **Microsoft Foundry path**: Tailored for data science and data engineering

> 💡 How to choose: **Start with your role, then pick the cert.** Don't force yourself through AI-103 if you're in a business role, and don't stop at Business Professional if you're an engineer.

## Tier 4 · Applied Skills Micro-Certifications (Free Badges)

This is where Microsoft's "cert-friendly" reputation really shines.

**Applied Skills** is Microsoft's free micro-certification program — **one skill, one certificate**, validated through an interactive online lab rather than a paid multiple-choice exam:

- **Zero cost**: Free to attempt, with free retakes after a 72-hour cooldown
- **Faster turnaround**: Roughly 45 minutes per lab, far quicker than full role-based exam prep
- **Concrete résumé proof**: Verifiable Microsoft credentials you can post directly on LinkedIn, with no expiration

If you want to quickly build up a stack of verifiable AI credentials without paying exam fees, Applied Skills offers the best return on your time.

## FAQ

### Are Microsoft Learn AI certifications completely free?

All self-paced courses, official practice assessments, and **Microsoft Applied Skills** micro-credentials on Microsoft Learn are 100% free. Only traditional proctored certification exams—such as AI-901 ($99 US standard) and AI-103 ($165 US standard, regional pricing applies)—require an exam fee via Pearson VUE.

### Can I still register for AI-900 or AI-102 in 2026?

No. Both AI-900 and AI-102 retired on June 30, 2026. AI-900 has been replaced by **AI-901** (*Azure AI Fundamentals*), and AI-102 has been replaced by **AI-103** (*Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate*). Verify the updated exam code before studying or scheduling.

### What is the difference between Microsoft Applied Skills and traditional certifications?

Applied Skills is "one skill, one cert": free, unproctored (~45-minute live lab), scenario-specific, and non-expiring. Traditional role-based certifications (like AI-901 and AI-103) are paid proctored exams covering broader job roles; Associate-level certs require a free annual online renewal assessment.

### Which Microsoft AI path should non-technical or business roles start with?

Start with the free one-hour *Fundamentals of Generative AI* module, move into the no-code **AI Business Professional** path or **AI-901** self-paced course, and earn one or two free Applied Skills labs in Copilot or business AI workflows before deciding whether you need a paid fundamentals exam.
