## FAQ

### Are these AI courses really completely free?

Yes. All courses indexed here are official free offerings from the 10 platforms. The only paid item mentioned is Anthropic's optional $99 certification exam — the learning material itself is free. No credit card is required for any of the courses.

### Which platform should a complete beginner start with?

Google Skills (Google AI Essentials) is the friendliest entry point: gamified paths, no coding required, and you earn a LinkedIn-ready Skill Badge. After that, OpenAI's ChatGPT 101/102 covers everyday workflows.

### Which free AI certificate is most worth getting?

For developers, Anthropic's Claude Certified Architect ($99 exam, free prep material) carries the strongest signal in 2026. For career switchers, Microsoft Applied Skills issues one credential per skill and is the fastest way to stack verifiable credentials at zero cost.
