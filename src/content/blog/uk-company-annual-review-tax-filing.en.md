## FAQ

### What is a UK Dormant Accounts filing?

When a company has had no significant financial transactions during an accounting period, it can file as dormant with both Companies House and HMRC. The process is much simpler than a standard tax return. That said, if your company has had any real transactions, dormant status doesn't apply.

### Are the annual review and tax filing the same thing?

No. The Confirmation Statement (annual review) is filed with Companies House to confirm your company's details. The tax filing (CT600 or Dormant Accounts) is submitted to HMRC. They're separate obligations with separate deadlines — you need to do both.
