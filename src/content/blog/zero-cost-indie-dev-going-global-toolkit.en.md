# Zero-Cost Global Launch Toolkit for Indie Developers

For indie developers, keeping costs under control is one of the most critical things you can do in the early stages of going global. By leveraging a variety of free tools and services, you can push your startup costs down to nearly zero.

From domain registration, website hosting, databases, authentication, payments, and email to SEO tools — there are now free or ultra-low-cost options available across the board. The key is combining these tools into a complete tech stack so you can stay focused on your product and marketing, rather than being weighed down by infrastructure costs.

Get your MVP off the ground with free-tier solutions first, then gradually upgrade to paid services once revenue starts coming in.
