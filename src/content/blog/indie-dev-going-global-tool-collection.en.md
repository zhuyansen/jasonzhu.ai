# Essential Tools for Indie Developers Going Global

Recommended time allocation across the three phases — **40% - 20% - 40%**

## Phase 1: Discovery — Market Research

### 1. Inspiration & Mining
- Product Hunt
- Toolify
- Twitter
- Reddit
- GitHub Trending

### 2. Trends & Search Heat
- Google Trends
- Keyword Planner

### 3. Competitor Analysis
- SimilarWeb
- SEMrush
- Ahrefs

## Phase 2: Build — Product Development

- Next.js / Nuxt.js frameworks
- Vercel / Cloudflare Pages for deployment
- Supabase / Firebase for backend
- Stripe / Lemon Squeezy for payments

## Phase 3: Growth — Marketing

- SEO (Google Search Console)
- Twitter / X social media
- Product Hunt launch
- Reddit community outreach
- Email marketing (Resend / Mailgun)

The core idea: spend 40% of your energy on market research and another 40% on marketing — product development only needs 20%. Most indie developers make the mistake of pouring 80% of their time into building. That's the single most common trap to avoid.
