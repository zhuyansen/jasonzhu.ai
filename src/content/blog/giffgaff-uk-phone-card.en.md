When building a cross-border business, forming an overseas company, or opening international SaaS and payment accounts from mainland China, a real carrier mobile number (`+44`) quickly becomes core infrastructure. ### 2. Choosing a Compatible Phone

A **US-model (or Hong Kong / international) unlocked phone** is the most reliable choice for keeping your overseas SIM isolated or using eSIM later. Since iPhones are pricey as a backup device, I bought a **second-hand Google Pixel 3 on Pinduoduo for ¥485**.

Check three hardware specs before buying:

- **Factory unlocked**: Confirm no US carrier lock (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile).

- **Native eSIM hardware (optional)**: Most mainland-China phones lack eSIM hardware. Dial `*#06#`—if an **EID** appears, native eSIM is supported. giffgaff does not officially support third-party programmable eSIM adapters.

## Step-by-Step: Activating a Giffgaff Physical SIM in China

1. **Prepare internet & payment**: Use a stable internet connection plus a Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or PayPal account able to pay in GBP.
2. **Open `giffgaff.com/activate`**: Enter the **6-digit activation code** (or 19-digit SIM serial) and register with a long-term email address you check regularly.
3. **Skip the monthly plan and select PAYG credit**: Scroll to the bottom of the plan selector, choose **"Pay as you go"** / **"I don't want a plan"**, and top up the **£10 minimum credit**.
4. **Check Auto-renew / Auto-top-up**: Confirm both are disabled in Payment settings if you want strict manual control.
5. **Wait for provisioning & insert SIM**: Activation usually finishes in **30–60 minutes** (up to **24 hours**), after which your `+44` number appears on your dashboard.
6. **Turn OFF Mobile Data & Data Roaming immediately**: Disable cellular data, roaming, and data switching on the giffgaff line so background sync at 20p/MB never drains your £10 balance.
7. **Toggle Airplane Mode**: Toggle Airplane Mode for 15 seconds so the phone attaches to China Mobile or China Unicom, then test incoming SMS.

If you have an eSIM-capable international phone, watch three official 2026 rules before activating in the app:

New eSIM sign-ups require buying a one-month non-contract pink PAYG plan first (from £6), then disabling **Auto-renew** and adding **£10 PAYG credit** for China keep-alive texts.
2. **UK Processing Window (4:30 AM – 9:30 PM UK Time)**: eSIM provisioning pauses overnight in the UK. From China (UTC+8), activate between **12:30 PM and 4:30 AM** to stay safely inside both GMT and BST windows.
3. **Do NOT Swap a Working Physical SIM to eSIM From China**: Official giffgaff Help warns that overseas SIM-to-eSIM swaps often fail 2FA security checks, and starting the swap immediately deactivates your old SIM. Keep a working physical SIM physical.

## The 6-Month Keep-Alive Rule: How Not to Lose Your UK Number

Under giffgaff Terms (§13.5–13.6), any SIM with **no qualifying usage for 6 months (~180–186 days)** is permanently deactivated.

**Common mistake**: assuming free incoming OTPs keep the line alive. **They do not.** Receiving texts/calls, 0800 calls, and Wi-Fi apps (WhatsApp) do not reset the timer. You must complete at least **one chargeable action**:

- **Send 1 outgoing SMS** (30p in China — cheapest and cleanest; text your own `+86` mobile).
- Make 1 outgoing call (£1/min), use PAYG data (20p/MB — riskier due to background apps), or top up credit (£10 min).

Sending one 30p SMS every **5 months** costs **60p–90p/year**, letting your £10 activation credit last over a decade. giffgaff emails warnings **1 month** and **~5 days** before deactivation. Once disconnected, the SIM and balance cannot be restored; you have only **30 days** to ask an agent for a **PAC code** to port your number to another UK network before permanent recycling.

## Troubleshooting Giffgaff in China

- **No signal**: Restart, toggle Airplane Mode, then open **Mobile Network → Network Operators**, disable Automatic Selection, and manually pick **China Mobile** or **China Unicom**.
- **No incoming SMS**: Verify your `+44` number is live on `giffgaff.com/dashboard`. Remember **Wi-Fi Calling does not work outside the UK**—you need cellular signal bars on a local partner tower.
- **Credit draining**: Confirm **Cellular Data**, **Data Roaming**, and **Allow Data Switching** are OFF on the giffgaff line, and ignore unknown calls (incoming calls/voicemail cost £1/min).
- **Slow activation**: Wait up to 24 hours (or until 4:30 AM UK time for overnight eSIM requests) before messaging an agent.

## FAQ

### Can I activate a giffgaff UK SIM card in China without going to the UK?

Yes. You can activate an unactivated physical giffgaff SIM from China online at `giffgaff.com/activate` using a Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or PayPal payment for a minimum £10 Pay As You Go credit top-up. You do not need to be in the UK or buy a monthly plan.

### Does receiving free SMS in China keep my giffgaff number active?

No. Although incoming SMS is completely free (£0) while roaming in China, receiving texts or calls does not count as qualifying activity under giffgaff's 6-month inactivity policy. To prevent permanent deactivation, send at least one chargeable outgoing SMS (30p) every 5–6 months.

### Should overseas founders in China choose a giffgaff physical SIM or eSIM in 2026?

If you already have access to a blank physical SIM (~¥15 via secondary channels), physical SIM is usually simpler and cheaper for China-based founders because it activates directly with £10 PAYG credit and avoids overseas eSIM swap restrictions. Starting July 27, 2026, activating a brand-new eSIM in the giffgaff app requires purchasing a one-month plan first (from £6), disabling auto-renew, and then topping up PAYG credit for China roaming.

### Do I need a giffgaff UK phone number to open Wise Business or Stripe for a UK Ltd?

For Wise Business, a UK number is optional—mainland mobile numbers work for initial verification and Wise recommends app-based or Authenticator 2FA, while KYC focuses on your real trading address. For a UK Stripe account, however, Stripe's cross-border setup expects a local UK business contact number, making a real non-VoIP carrier line like giffgaff far more reliable than virtual VoIP numbers.
