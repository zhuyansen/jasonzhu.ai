## Why Do You Need an Overseas SIM Card?

When expanding internationally, having a foreign SIM card is pretty much a necessity. After registering my UK company, I went ahead and applied for a GiffGaff UK SIM card.

## How to Get One

GiffGaff's official website no longer offers China as a shipping destination, so I bought mine through Xiaohongshu (RedNote) for around **¥15**. I'd recommend picking one up sooner rather than later — hard to say whether that'll still be an option down the road.

## Choosing a Phone

A US-model phone is the way to go. iPhones are pricey, so I picked up a second-hand Google Pixel 3 on Pinduoduo for **¥485**.

## GiffGaff Activation Steps

1. Once your SIM card arrives, head to the GiffGaff website to begin activation
2. Insert the SIM card into a compatible phone
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete registration and activation
4. Choose a plan or top up your balance

## Tips

- Have a compatible phone ready before your SIM card arrives
- A stable internet connection is required during activation
- GiffGaff offers flexible plans — pick whatever suits your needs
