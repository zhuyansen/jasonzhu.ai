title: Storytelling — Turning User Interest Into Belief
excerpt: Storytelling is a core skill in both marketing and product development — it's what turns user interest into genuine belief.
---

Storytelling turns user interest into belief.

That's true in product. That's true in marketing.

Users don't open their wallets because of a feature list. They buy in because a story resonates with them. A good story takes abstract value and makes it concrete — it transforms "that seems pretty good" into "I absolutely have to have this." Whether you're building products or running marketing campaigns, learning to tell compelling stories is the most foundational skill there is.
