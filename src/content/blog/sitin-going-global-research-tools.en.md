A closer look at three must-have research tools for going global:

## 1. AITDK
A browser extension that displays key metrics for any website directly in your browser — including traffic sources, keyword rankings, and SEO difficulty scores. It's one of the most frequently used tools in the toolkit for anyone building a global product.

## 2. SEOquake
A battle-tested SEO extension that lets you instantly check a page's keyword density, meta information, and indexing status with a single click. It's incredibly handy for SEO analysis, giving you a fast read on the SEO health of any competitor's page.

## 3. Wappalyzer
A technology detection tool that identifies the tech stack behind any website in one click — including frameworks, CMS platforms, analytics tools, payment solutions, and more. It helps you understand the technical choices your competitors have made, giving you useful reference points for your own stack decisions.

Used together, these three extensions cover the core needs of global market research: traffic analysis, SEO diagnostics, and tech stack identification. Once installed, you can pull up any competitor's website and get the critical insights you need in seconds.
