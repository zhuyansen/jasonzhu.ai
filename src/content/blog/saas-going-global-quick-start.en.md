If you want to start with what you're best at in SaaS or indie development, **begin with a wrapper**.

Ship something quickly, and you'll quickly realize what you're lacking — **marketing**.

Here's the practical stuff to get your first project off the ground:

## 1. Landing Page Templates
- Free template resources
- 程普 @weijunext's Nexty (paid, full-stack SaaS template — the landing pages look great and are highly versatile; swap out the copy and images and it's yours)
- Other paid templates

## Core Advice

Ship something "ugly" first. Don't chase perfection — get it live, get it moving.

Templates only solve 10% of the problem. The real bottlenecks are:
- **Stripe payments** (not supported in many countries)
- **SEO cold start**

Before you build a wrapper, verify that your target market has a working payment channel. Otherwise, no matter how beautiful your landing page looks, you won't collect a dime.

The biggest challenge with wrappers isn't technical — it's pricing. The real moat for indie developers isn't speed; it's finding the niche use cases that big companies can't be bothered to build for.
