## Background

With the whole One Person Company (OPC) trend picking up steam in China, signing contracts should be as painless as possible.

## Research Method

I had Manus AI put together a comprehensive side-by-side comparison of e-signature platforms.

## Four Domestic Platforms

| Platform | Highlights | Best For |
|----------|------------|----------|
| **e签宝** | #1 market share | Medium to large enterprises |
| **腾讯电子签** | Deeply integrated with WeChat ecosystem — sign via mini program | SMEs |
| **契约锁** | Strong private deployment options | State-owned and central enterprises |
| Other platforms | Each has its own strengths | Choose based on specific needs |

## Overseas Options

For businesses expanding globally, you also need to think about the signing experience for international clients. SignNow is a solid, cost-effective choice here.

## Final Decision

In the end, I kept it lean and went with:

- **腾讯电子签** — for domestic signing scenarios
- **SignNow** — for overseas signing scenarios

## Recommendations

- If your clients are primarily in China, 腾讯电子签's WeChat ecosystem integration is a clear advantage
- If you need to handle international signings, SignNow offers much better value than DocuSign
- Large enterprises should take a closer look at e签宝
- Companies with strict data security requirements may want to explore 契约锁's private deployment option
