Ban @xbanboo's Marketing Playbook

**1. Mutual Win**

A great partner isn't something you stumble upon overnight.

Before committing to any collaboration, do your due diligence.

When working with someone, always ask: is there a deal structure where everyone comes out ahead?

**2. Communication**

One-on-one? Lead with sincerity.

One-to-many? Lead with presence.

Marketing isn't about pushing your agenda onto someone else — it's about creating a situation where both sides genuinely benefit. Finding the right partner starts with validating each other's value, then designing an arrangement that works for everyone at the table. And how you communicate has to match the context: authenticity wins in private conversations, while clarity and delivery win in front of a crowd.
