Every week I count the websites that just put up a checkout link on one of 7 payment platforms (Stripe, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Creem, Dodo, Paddle), to see what people are selling, what is taking off, and which needs nobody is serving yet.

## This week in numbers

- The link graph picked up **6,457 new sellers** this week: 1,143 sell software and 635 sell AI products.
- Software was 20% of new sellers in September, so one in five new sellers now sells software. Through 2025 that share sat at 6–8%.
- The newer payment platforms keep growing: Polar added 200 sellers this week, Dodo 58, Creem 32. 71% of Dodo's sellers sell software.

> Single-week numbers swing a lot: the link graph indexes new links in batches, and earlier weeks ranged from 1,100 to 4,300. Look at monthly numbers for trends.

## 5 new products worth a look

1. **RunVouch** (runvouch.com, paid via Polar): a watchdog for unattended AI agents. It proves a scheduled job actually finished and alerts you the moment one doesn't. The more agents people run, the more demand there is for "agent ops" tools like this.
2. **Garnet** (garnetgrid.com): a private AI box that lives in your own office. One machine, and you can pull the network cable. The pitch is that data never leaves the building, aimed at small law firms, clinics and others that won't touch the cloud.
3. **PixelGlass** (pixelglass.co): write posts, send newsletters and sell memberships in a proper editor, with Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor helping on the side. Payments via Stripe, Paystack or Dodo.
4. **BetterTracker** (bettertracker.com): finds wasted SaaS subscriptions and shadow IT for companies and managed IT providers. A related play: a set of tools that only hunt Microsoft 365 waste, with seats, storage and Copilot each sold as its own product on its own domain.
5. **Depix** (depix.ai): an AI design platform for industrial designers that turns a concept into a realistic 3D render in minutes.

## Fastest-growing new sites, last three months

(Traffic is GoAnyAPI's estimate, July → September)

- **rewind.ai**: 178K → 459K. The domain used to belong to the AI app Rewind (the company renamed itself Limitless in 2024). Someone re-registered it in May and turned it into an AI tools directory, and it still ranks for "rewind ai". **Picking up a well-known expired domain can inherit its search traffic outright.**
- **undetectr.com**: 79K → 187K. Keeps AI-generated music from being detected by platforms.
- **ottermind.ai**: 11K → 98K. Hand an idea to an AI agent and get the finished thing back.
- **grandranker.com**: 25K → 91K. A fully automated AI SEO platform.

## Demand we verified

Checked against 12 months of US Google Trends data, these really are rising:

| Keyword | Monthly searches | Difficulty | 12-month trend | Paying sellers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hermes agent | 74K | 38 | Peaked in Apr–May, now steady at 16% of peak | 4 |
| ai coding agent | 8,100 | 27 | ×2.2 | 45 |
| social media scheduler | 90.5K | 33 | ×1.8 | 3 |
| athlete tracker | 49.5K | 22 | ×2.1 | 1 |

The long tail of hermes agent is very easy: hermes agent github (2,900, difficulty 9), hermes agent vs openclaw (1,300, difficulty 7), hermes skills (880, difficulty 11).

**A trap to avoid**: many of the "year-over-year explosion" numbers in keyword tools are fake. engineering software shows +10,285% and visio software +2,505%, but Trends says both are flat or falling. The YoY figure jumps whenever Google Ads regroups keywords. **When you see a spike, check it in Trends first.**

## Playbook of the week: one tool, one exact-match domain per language

One AdSense account runs 10 sites, mostly two tools: background removal and image compression, plus a map image generator and a comic maker. Each tool gets its own site in German, Spanish, Dutch and English, and the domain is the local word for the task, for example the German for "remove background".

This works on free-tool keywords with huge demand and almost nobody charging: searches often run into the hundreds of thousands, but few sellers take payment because the money comes from ads. Keep in mind that Google has been cracking down on scaled content for two years now. The cruder the copy, the bigger the risk.

## Payment platform news

- **Lemon Squeezy**: in January the CEO publicly admitted support had slowed and updates had thinned out, and the team is steering users toward Stripe Managed Payments. Think twice before choosing it for a new project.
- **Polar**: since May 27, new organizations pay 5% + 50¢.
- **Creem**: closed a €5M seed round in September and lets individuals in mainland China withdraw to Alipay.
- **Dodo Payments**: added 106 sellers in September, four times May's number; 70% of its sellers sell software.
