Black Friday Premium deal tutorial (regional price comparison + full walkthrough)

## Requirements

The Black Friday discount must be gifted by someone else. The recipient cannot already be a Premium subscriber, and the sender must be a Premium subscriber. Additionally, the regional pricing is locked to whichever region the sender used when they originally subscribed — it cannot be changed.

## Option 1: Don't Have an Account

You can create a secondary account, subscribe for one month, and then gift it to yourself or ask a friend to help.
