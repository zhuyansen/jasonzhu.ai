Previously, some followers asked about the training syllabus. It's roughly five sessions:

1. Understanding AI, the importance of streamlining your AI news sources and information intake, and hands-on use of various text-generation tools

2. Prompt engineering, image generation, and hands-on use of various video-generation tools

3. Using AI automation tools

4. Real-world implementation — live, on-site demonstration of building Agents in LinkedIn and Feishu tailored to the client company's specific scenarios

5. Providing skill-based recommendations for the client's various workflows, then listening to their use cases on the spot and delivering targeted solutions
