I sat in on A-Meng's overseas SaaS website breakdown today, and it was genuinely eye-opening.

Unlike me — I spent a month on an overseas site, got zero traction, and bailed for content creation.

**AI video / shell sites aren't about "building a site." They're about product-led operations:**

- Traffic is just the top of the funnel. What really counts is **conversion funnel + LTV + iteration + risk control**.

## Three Key Takeaways

1. **Don't trust your gut — trust your funnel data.** Every conversion step needs to be backed by numbers: registration rate, payment rate, retention rate. Find where users are dropping off and fix that step.

2. **Focus on customer lifetime value (LTV).** Don't just look at first-purchase revenue — look at how long users keep paying. Your LTV determines how much you can afford to spend on acquisition.

3. **Don't sleep on risk management.** Payment fraud, content compliance, API cost fluctuations — all of these hit your margins directly and can't be an afterthought.

Launching an overseas SaaS product isn't the finish line. The real challenge starts after launch, in the ongoing work of operating and optimizing.
