> **Fact-checked November 26, 2026.** Vendor certification catalogs changed heavily in 2026: Microsoft retired **AI-900** and **AI-102** on June 30, 2026 (replacing them with **AI-901** and **AI-103**), NVIDIA moved several classic deep learning and RAG courses to $90 while keeping a rotating set of free DLI courses, and both OpenAI and Anthropic restricted their formal proctored certifications to eligible enterprise or partner organizations.

If you are deciding where to spend your study hours—and whether any AI certification is actually free and open to individuals—this side-by-side guide breaks down the five vendors searchers compare most often.

## Vendor-by-Vendor Breakdown (Verified Late 2026)

### 1. OpenAI: OpenAI Academy vs. OpenAI Certified

OpenAI separates its public learning portal from its formal certification track—and confusing the two is the most common mistake job seekers make in 2026:

- **OpenAI Academy (`academy.openai.com`)**: Free to anyone with a ChatGPT account. Covers four pathways: applying AI at work (ChatGPT 101/102, prompting, workflows), building with Codex and the OpenAI API, leading enterprise AI adoption, and teaching/education. Passing course assessments (80%+) grants a digital badge or pathway completion certificate, **but OpenAI's Help Center explicitly states these are not formal OpenAI Certifications**.
- **OpenAI Certified**: Built with Coursera, ETS, and Credly and delivered as an interactive app inside ChatGPT. Instead of pure multiple-choice exams, learners complete hands-on tasks (starting with *AI Foundations*). As of late 2026, access remains **invite-only for eligible ChatGPT Enterprise and ChatGPT Edu workspaces**, with no public consumer exam checkout. *(OpenAI also hosts a public Coursera course specifically for educators: ChatGPT Foundations for Teachers.)*

### 2. Anthropic: Free Developer Courses vs. Claude Certified Architect

Anthropic offers arguably the strongest free developer curriculum for building real agentic workflows (Claude API, Model Context Protocol / MCP, Claude Code, and Skills), paired with a five-exam professional certification suite:

- **Public Courses (Claude Academy)**: Free and open to everyone, issuing completion certificates that verify course participation.
- **Claude Certification Program (CPN-Only)**: Launched alongside Anthropic's Claude Partner Network (CPN) in 2026 and delivered via Pearson VUE with Credly badges valid for **12 months**. The lineup spans **Associate – Foundations (`CCAO-F`, $99 list)**, **Developer – Foundations (`CCDV-F`, $125)**, **Developer – Professional (`CCDV-P`, $175)**, **Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (`CCAR-F`, $125)**, and **Architect – Professional (`CCAR-P`, $175)**.
- **The Eligibility Gate**: You **must** register with a recognized company email from a Claude Partner Network member organization. If you are an independent developer or outside CPN, take the free courses for the engineering skills and ship a public GitHub project instead of hunting for an exam voucher.

👉 Deep dive: [Claude Certified Architect Complete Roadmap](/en/blog/anthropic-claude-certified-architect-roadmap) and [OpenAI Academy vs. Anthropic Comparison](/en/blog/openai-academy-vs-anthropic-learning-paths)

### 3. Microsoft: The Most Accessible Public Credential Ladder

If you want a recognized Big Tech AI credential that any individual can actually earn today, Microsoft has the cleanest two-tier ladder:

- **Microsoft Applied Skills (100% Free)**: On-demand interactive lab assessments (typically ~45 minutes) verifying one concrete task at a time—such as developing agents in **Microsoft Foundry**, building a generative AI chat app, securing cloud AI solutions, or automating workflows with Copilot Studio. Zero exam fees, verifiable directly on your Microsoft Learn transcript.
- **Proctored Certifications (Major 2026 Exam Refresh)**: Microsoft retired **AI-900** and **AI-102** on **June 30, 2026**:
  - **AI-901** replaces AI-900 for *Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Fundamentals* ($99 US standard, passing score 700/1000). Unlike the purely conceptual AI-900, AI-901 expects basic Python familiarity plus hands-on knowledge of Microsoft Foundry and REST APIs/SDKs.
  - **AI-103** replaces AI-102 for *Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate* (passing score 700/1000, free annual online renewal), heavily weighted toward generative AI, RAG, tool-calling agents, and Foundry governance.

👉 Step-by-step breakdown: [Microsoft Learn AI Certifications Complete Guide](/en/blog/microsoft-learn-ai-certifications)

### 4. NVIDIA: Proctored LLM Exams & Rotating Free DLI Courses

NVIDIA's Deep Learning Institute (DLI) is **partly free**, not all-free, so know what costs money before you start:

- **Free Tier**: Around **10 rotating self-paced DLI courses** (currently concentrated in Agentic AI, agent security, Omniverse, OpenUSD, and robotics), plus NVIDIA's free **open-source *Fundamentals of Deep Learning*** course on GitHub (Apache 2.0 / CC BY 4.0, bring your own GPU, **no certificate**).
- **Paid DLI Courses**: Core hands-on GPU-backed courses—such as *Building RAG Agents with LLMs* and *Fundamentals of Accelerated Computing with CUDA Python*—cost **$30** (2–4 hours) or **$90** (8 hours, includes a cloud GPU workstation and a course certificate upon passing the assessment). University educators in the DLI Teaching Kit program can request free course codes for students.
- **Proctored Certifications**: Open globally via Certiverse and valid for **2 years** (recertification requires retaking the paid exam). The flagship entry-level LLM exam is **NCA-GENL** (*NVIDIA-Certified Associate: Generative AI LLMs*, **$125 USD**, 60 minutes, 50–60 questions), while Professional exams such as **NCP-GENL** cost **$200–$500 USD**.

👉 Full free vs. paid catalog audit: [NVIDIA DLI Free Courses & Certification Guide (2026)](/en/blog/nvidia-dli-free-courses)

### 5. IBM SkillsBuild: Best $0 Verifiable Credly Badge for Beginners

IBM SkillsBuild hosts **1,000+ free courses** and issues **official Credly digital badges** at zero cost—making it the strongest completely free resume credential for beginners, students, and career switchers:

- **Flagship Badge**: **Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals** (~10 hours across 6 modules covering AI concepts, NLP/vision, ML/DL, a Watson Studio hands-on simulation, AI ethics, and AI careers—plus IBM's refreshed two-part AI Fundamentals track expanding into generative AI and AI agents). Pass the assessments (80%+) and claim your badge on Credly using the same email address.
- **Critical Signup Warning**: When registering at `skillsbuild.org`, select **Adult Learners** if you are 18+ and want the full catalog. Choosing the wrong learner profile hides roughly half of the AI catalog. Immediately run the **AI Level Up** assessment to get a focused study plan instead of getting lost in 1,000+ courses.

## Four Mistakes That Waste Time (and Money) in 2026

1. **Treating completion badges as proctored certifications**: OpenAI Academy badges and public Claude Academy certificates show you finished a course, not that you passed a proctored exam. Be honest on your resume—pair free courses with a live project or a verifiable lab credential like Microsoft Applied Skills.
2. **Buying outdated AI-900 or AI-102 prep bundles**: Both Microsoft exams retired on June 30, 2026. Check that any study guide explicitly targets **AI-901** (which now expects light Python + Microsoft Foundry) or **AI-103** (agentic Azure AI apps).
3. **Paying $200+ for third-party "AI certificate bootcamps"**: Between OpenAI Academy, Anthropic's public developer track, Microsoft Learn, IBM SkillsBuild, and NVIDIA's open-source course, the official primary sources are free. Spend money only on recognized proctored exam fees ($99–$175) when a target employer actually values them.
4. **Skipping hands-on proof**: Hiring managers for AI engineering roles weigh a working MCP tool, RAG pipeline, or deployed agent far higher than a stack of beginner badges. Use certifications to structure your study—then build and ship.

## FAQ

### Does OpenAI, Anthropic, or Microsoft offer official AI certifications in 2026?

Microsoft offers official AI certifications open to the public—including free **Microsoft Applied Skills** interactive labs and paid proctored exams (**AI-901** at $99 US standard and **AI-103** for Azure AI app and agent developers). OpenAI and Anthropic also run formal certification programs, but neither is open to general public registration as of November 2026: **OpenAI Certified** (via Coursera inside ChatGPT) is invite-only for eligible ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu workspaces, and **Anthropic's Claude Certified** exams (including Claude Certified Architect) are restricted to employees of Claude Partner Network member organizations.

### What is the best completely free AI certification with a verifiable badge in 2026?

For beginners, students, and career switchers, **IBM SkillsBuild Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals** (~10 hours) awards a completely free, verifiable **Credly digital badge**. For developers and technical builders, **Microsoft Applied Skills** offers completely free interactive lab credentials (~45 minutes each) covering Microsoft Foundry, AI agents, and cloud AI security that verify directly on your Microsoft Learn transcript.

### Is NVIDIA NCA-GENL worth $125 compared to Microsoft AI-901 or AI-103?

Choose based on your target tech stack. **Microsoft AI-901** ($99) and **AI-103** carry broader recognition across corporate IT and Azure enterprise teams, and Microsoft offers free annual renewal on role-based certifications. **NVIDIA NCA-GENL** ($125, valid for 2 years with paid recertification) is a stronger fit if you specifically target LLM engineering, GPU-accelerated ML infrastructure, or teams building heavily on NVIDIA's AI stack.

### Can I still take Microsoft AI-900 or AI-102?

No. Microsoft retired both **AI-900** and **AI-102** on June 30, 2026. Register for **AI-901** (*Azure AI Fundamentals*) if you want the entry-level credential, or **AI-103** (*Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate*) if you are an engineer building production generative AI and agentic applications on Azure.
