Three things you should absolutely embrace in 2026:

## 1. The Future Belongs to Agents — Embrace the CLI

GUIs are built for humans. Even if you're not rebuilding your product from scratch, it needs to support CLI. That's where things are heading.

## 2. Think Like a Manager

Most people are used to being in the weeds. You need to manage AI the same way you'd manage people — with a manager's mindset. If you're still obsessing over execution-level details, you're the one getting in AI's way.
