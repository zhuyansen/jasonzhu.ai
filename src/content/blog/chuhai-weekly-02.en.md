Every week I count the websites that just put up a checkout link on one of 7 payment platforms (Stripe, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Creem, Dodo, Paddle), to see what people are selling, what is taking off, and which needs nobody is serving yet.

## This week in numbers

- The link graph picked up **6,152 new sellers** this week: 1,069 sell software and 596 sell AI products.
- Monthly numbers are steadier: the first 18 days of October already brought 12.4K new sellers, more than September's full-month 11K. Software stayed at about 20% of new sellers in both months and did not climb further.
- With October not even over, the newer payment platforms have already passed their September totals: Polar 358 (September 263), Lemon Squeezy 572 (431), Dodo 111 (107), Creem 55 (44).

> Single-week numbers swing a lot: the link graph indexes new links in batches, and we have seen anywhere from 1,100 to 6,500 a week. Look at monthly numbers for trends.

## 5 new products worth a look

1. **1VideoTool** (1videotool.com, paid via Lemon Squeezy): a video studio for Mac or Windows that coding agents like Claude Code and Codex drive over MCP. One sentence gets you a finished video with voiceover and music, and the files stay on your machine. The free tier exports 25 videos; Pro is a one-time $29 to $89.
2. **Catenary** (thecatenary.app): an IDE for coding agents, with the editor, terminal and several agents in one window and a separate branch and worktree for each task. Pro is a one-time $19.
3. **Memora** (memora.aircoded.app, paid via Polar): records meetings on a Mac (microphone plus system audio), transcribes locally with Whisper without uploading any audio, and exports Markdown for Obsidian. One-time €29.
4. **ReceiptRadar** (receiptradar.belkins.app): forward receipts to an inbox and it reads them, sorts them into QuickBooks accounts and pushes them as entries waiting for approval. Aimed at small businesses and bookkeepers handling 50 to 500 receipts a month, $149/month, still in presale.
5. **GumLeaf** (getgumleaf.com): text a question and get an answer back, including over satellite SMS, so you can ask about weather, first aid or water safety with no internet. For hikers, campers and people on basic phones. $4.99/month.

## Playbook of the week: tools around coding agents, sold as one-time purchases

Among this week's new software sellers, about 50 products name Claude Code, Codex or MCP right in their description. They don't build agents; they build what goes around them:

- **Multi-agent workspaces**: terminals, boards and IDEs that put several agents in one window, each in its own worktree.
- **Cleaning up after agents**: clearing the caches and media agents leave on your Mac, rolling back sessions, tracking quota usage.
- **Giving agents new abilities**: video, email, database schemas and memory, packaged as MCP servers agents can call.
- **Config file generators**: answer a few questions and get a CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md.

Many are local-first one-time purchases priced between $19 and $89. They target specific pain points of agent users, and the search terms are new with little competition. The risk is that the agent platforms can build the feature in themselves at any time.

## Payment platform news

- **Dodo Payments**: the October 12 release added combined checkout for multiple subscriptions (one invoice, each renewing on its own), plus GCash (Philippines), AlipayHK and FPS (Hong Kong), Touch 'n Go (Malaysia) and Apple Pay in India. [Source](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/newsletter-october12)
- **Polar**: an October 8 post says the product will be rebuilt as a foundation for metering, entitlements and billing, aimed at usage-based and agent products. That includes local metering, pricing defined in code, and treating humans and machines as one kind of customer. No release date yet; they are recruiting design partners. [Source](https://polar.sh/blog/building-the-next-evolution-of-polar)
