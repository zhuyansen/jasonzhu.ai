Short Answer (Key Takeaways)

Non-UK residents and independent software founders can register a UK Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd) directly through Companies House online in roughly two hours—no UK citizenship or local residency required. Total first-day startup expense consists of the official Companies House online incorporation fee (£100 since February 1, 2026, up from the legacy £50 rate) plus a compliant UK registered office address service (~$50–$60/year, or roughly ¥400 RMB), cutting out $400–$800 formation-agency markups. Once incorporated, founders typically pair the company with Wise Business and Stripe, keeping ongoing annual compliance predictable.

1. Why Many International Indie Founders Choose a UK Ltd Over a US LLC

Before deciding where to incorporate for global SaaS billing, non-US software makers compare two dominant low-friction jurisdictions: a US Wyoming LLC and a UK Private Limited Company (Ltd). While a Wyoming LLC provides native US banking and Apple/Google US corporate presence, foreign-owned single-member US LLCs carry strict annual IRS Form 5472 / pro-forma Form 1120 reporting penalties ($25,000+ for missed filings) that frequently force solo founders to hire specialized US CPAs.

A UK Limited Company avoids US federal disregarded-entity filing complexity while unlocking fast online government incorporation, reputable European/international corporate credibility, low-cost multi-currency banking via Wise Business, and seamless Stripe UK/global onboarding—all without traveling to London.

2. Direct Companies House Filing Walkthrough (Skipping Expensive Agents)

Commercial formation intermediaries advertise attractive headline prices that quickly multiply once registered-office mail forwarding, statutory registers, and "banking concierge" bundles are toggled on at checkout. As shown in the JasonZhu.AI UK company 2-hour filing guide, solo builders can bypass inflated agency upsells almost completely by breaking setup into three clean steps:

  1. Secure a compliant UK registered office address first: Every UK company needs an official physical UK address on the public Companies House register where statutory government notices can be delivered (do not enter an unverified random street address). Low-cost virtual office providers supply a real UK street address and digital mail scanning for roughly £40–£60/year.
  2. Complete online incorporation directly on GOV.UK: Create a Companies House / GOV.UK One Login account, choose Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd), specify SIC codes matching software publishing or IT consulting (e.g., 62012 or 62090), enter genuine director passport/identity details and residential address, and issue simple ordinary shares (e.g., 100 shares at £1).
  3. Pay the £100 statutory filing fee and download incorporation PDFs: Retain your Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and company authentication code securely—you will need every PDF immediately when applying for Wise Business and Stripe.

3. Year-One Recurring Obligations New Founders Routinely Overlook

Incorporation day is straightforward, yet two recurring UK obligations catch non-resident founders off guard in Month 2 and Month 12:

  • UK ICO Data Protection Fee (£52/year tier): Software apps collecting customer emails, analytics, or billing details typically must register or pay the annual Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) fee unless exempt, taking ten minutes online.
  • Wise Business address verification: Opening Wise Business requires your registered UK address details to match Companies House exactly, plus clean proof connecting the UK company name and business operating address—misaligned documents stall account activation.
  • Annual Confirmation Statement & Accounts: Even dormant or low-revenue startups must file an annual Confirmation Statement (£34 online) and statutory accounts with Companies House and HMRC on time to avoid automatic late-filing fines starting at £150.

4. Pre-Registration Checklist Before Opening GOV.UK

Verify five items beforehand so the entire filing completes smoothly in one sitting: confirm your proposed company name isn't already taken or restricted on Companies House search; keep passport and residential proof matching accurately; confirm your virtual registered-office provider includes official government/HMRC mail forwarding (not solely generic commercial mail); keep an international Visa or Mastercard ready for the £100 fee; and prepare a simple corporate domain landing page before applying to Wise Business and Stripe.

Just as importantly, compare Year-2 renewal rates before signing with any third-party formation bundle: low introductory Year-1 offers frequently jump to £150–£300/year for mail forwarding and annual confirmation-statement handling—meaning founders who handle direct GOV.UK filings themselves often save hundreds of pounds annually while retaining direct control over company authentication codes and statutory tax deadlines. Additionally, keep your Persons with Significant Control (PSC) filings and software SIC classification consistent from Day 1, as payment processors verify Companies House shareholder percentages directly during underwriting, and register accurately with HMRC once active commercial trading begins.

FAQ

Can non-UK citizens legally own and direct 100% of a UK Limited Company?

Yes. UK company law imposes no citizenship or local residency requirement on company directors or shareholders. Non-UK founders worldwide routinely register and manage 100%-owned UK Ltd companies online.

How much does UK company formation actually cost in 2026?

Companies House raised its online incorporation fee from £50 to £100 effective February 1, 2026. Including a commercial UK registered office address service (~£40–£60/year), direct DIY incorporation typically totals around £140–£160 upfront.

Can I open Wise Business and Stripe right after receiving my UK Certificate of Incorporation?

Yes. Most solo software builders apply for Wise Business immediately after receiving Companies House incorporation documents and verifying address details, then connect those UK GBP/EUR/USD account rails directly into Stripe for global card processing.
