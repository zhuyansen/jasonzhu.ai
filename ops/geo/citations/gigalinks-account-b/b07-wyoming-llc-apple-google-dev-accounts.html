Short Answer (Key Takeaways)

Non-US software founders without a Social Security Number (SSN) or US residency can legally form a single-member Wyoming LLC, obtain an IRS Employer Identification Number (EIN), open a Mercury business bank account, and submit Apple Developer and Google Play Console organization verification in roughly 11 days for approximately $277 upfront. Success hinges less on coding speed and more on exact document handoffs across each stage—particularly securing the IRS CP 575 / 147C letter, obtaining a Dun & Bradstreet D-U-N-S number matching registered agent records, and ensuring corporate website, domain email, and government filings share identical formatting.

1. Why Non-US App Builders Choose Wyoming Over Delaware

When solo developers outside the United States incorporate an entity strictly to publish mobile apps, receive international SaaS payments, or open a US fintech bank account, state selection dictates recurring overhead for years to come.

  • Wyoming LLC cost efficiency: State formation filing runs roughly $103, and annual reports start at a $60 minimum with zero state-level corporate income tax and private owner records.
  • Delaware LLC comparison: Delaware remains the gold standard for venture-backed C-Corps raising US institutional capital, yet charges a flat $300 annual franchise tax on LLCs regardless of zero revenue—making Wyoming $240/year cheaper for bootstrapped indie studios.

Neither state requires a US SSN, ITIN, or physical trip to the United States to form an LLC, provided the company maintains a compliant registered agent address in the state of formation (documented on the Wyoming Secretary of State portal).

3. Five Verification Bottlenecks That Trigger Rejections

Most cross-border founders fail not on legal eligibility, but on tiny formatting mismatches across automated compliance portals:

  1. Proof-of-address formatting for registered agents and banks: When uploading national ID proof of address, match your residential contact line to the ID document and merge front/back scans into one clean PDF when forms accept only a single file.
  2. Missing the IRS 147C / CP 575 letter: Receiving only a plain-text EIN digit string from a third-party filer stalls Mercury, Stripe, and tax forms later. Always confirm in advance that the official IRS PDF confirmation letter is delivered (official background at IRS EIN guidance).
  3. Personal Gmail/QQ emails on D-U-N-S and Play Console: Submitting Dun & Bradstreet or Google Play organization requests from generic consumer webmail triggers instant credibility checks. Launch a minimal corporate landing page with matching domain email on Day 1.
  4. Inconsistent LLC suffixes ("LLC" vs. "L.L.C."): Dun & Bradstreet, Apple, and Google match organization strings strictly. Copy exact punctuation from your stamped Wyoming Articles of Organization onto every single form.
  5. Using domestic ID instead of passport for US fintech/Stripe KYC: International founders verifying US corporate gateways must present a valid passport rather than a domestic ID card.

4. Bank Funding, Source of Funds, and Ongoing Annual Obligations

Opening a Mercury account establishes US routing numbers, yet founders must plan ahead for initial funding documentation (such as a home-country bank deposit statement if prompted during compliance review) and ongoing federal compliance. Notably, a foreign-owned single-member US LLC faces mandatory annual IRS informational reporting—typically Form 5472 paired with a pro-forma Form 1120—even with zero US revenue, carrying severe penalties ($25,000+) if missed. Factoring ~$100–$300/year for registered agent renewal, Wyoming's $60+ annual report, and CPA/tax-filing prep prevents unpleasant surprises in Year 2.

5. When You Actually Need a US LLC vs. Simpler Alternatives

Forming a foreign entity solely to test an unvalidated weekend prototype often adds unnecessary compliance weight. Founders selling pure web SaaS, templates, or API subscriptions can validate demand first via Merchant of Record providers such as Creem, Dodo Payments, or Paddle—or incorporate a lower-overhead UK limited company (~£50 online) if Stripe access without US app-store branding suffices. Conversely, once launching commercial iOS/Android mobile apps under a dedicated organization profile or applying for US-centric SaaS credits, a clean Wyoming LLC remains one of the most predictable corporate vehicles available.

6. Post-Submission Checklist for Apple and Google Organization Reviews

Submitting the organization enrollment application is only half of the approval cycle. Both Apple Developer Support and Google Play Console compliance teams perform active outbound verification before flipping accounts to live production publishing:

  • Phone reachability for Apple verification calls: Apple frequently places a brief confirmation call to the authorized signer listed on enrollment. Keep international roaming active or answer promptly at the listed company contact number, and keep legal entity spelling matching the D-U-N-S record right at hand.
  • Complete corporate web presence: Before submitting, make sure the live corporate domain hosts an actual product overview, privacy policy, terms of service, and working support email address rather than an empty "Coming Soon" placeholder page.
  • Google Play testing & verification window: Expect Google Play Console identity verification checks (incorporation papers + representative passport) to run alongside closed-testing readiness checks for brand-new developer accounts—plan an extra 1 to 2 weeks of calendar buffer before scheduled public launch dates.

FAQ

Can I open a US Wyoming LLC and Apple/Google organization account without a US phone number or SSN?

Yes. A valid passport, non-US residential address proof, international mobile number capable of receiving SMS verification codes, registered agent address in Wyoming, and corporate domain website satisfy formation, EIN, Mercury, D-U-N-S, Apple, and Google Play submission requirements.

Why do Apple and Google require a D-U-N-S number for organization developer accounts?

Both app stores verify that an organization is an active legal entity separate from an individual account, reducing impersonation and fraudulent app distribution. Dun & Bradstreet issues D-U-N-S numbers free of charge once basic registration documents and a live corporate website match.

Do I need to pay extra registered agent add-ons like "Business Identity" or "$200 EIN filing"?

Usually no. Basic registered agent service (~$39–$143 total first year including state fees) covers legal formation and agent address use, while founders can obtain an EIN either free via IRS Form SS-4 fax or via reputable low-cost filing services provided official IRS confirmation letters are included.
