2. Step-by-Step Onboarding Flow (Registered vs. Trading Addresses)

Preparing exact registration artifacts prior to opening Wise Business eliminates multi-week compliance back-and-forth:

  1. Gather official UK Companies House PDFs: Download your Certificate of Incorporation, company number, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and exact shareholding details matching public Companies House entries.
  2. Separate Registered Office vs. Operating Business Address clearly: Wise distinguishes between your statutory UK registered office (for example, your registered virtual office service) and your actual trading or operating business address where day-to-day software work happens.
  3. Prepare personal director KYC & SMS verification: Verify the controlling director's passport and active international mobile number cleanly, avoiding mismatched residential spellings.
  4. Document product nature and public corporate website: Provide a live HTTPS domain showcasing your SaaS product, clear pricing, support contact, and privacy terms rather than an empty domain parking page.
  5. Complete the one-time setup payment: Once identity and corporate verification clear, pay the one-time business account activation fee to unlock local GBP, EUR, and USD receiving details.

3. How Address Verification Fails—and How to Pass Cleanly

The single sharpest friction point for new UK Ltd companies applying to Wise Business is address verification. Automated review teams reject utility bills or lease documents issued solely under a founder's personal name if requested for a corporate entity, or reject virtual-office invoices lacking the exact UK Limited Company name printed prominently on the document.

To clear address checks on the first submission: verify ahead of buying a virtual UK registered office package that official invoices or proof-of-address letters explicitly print your full UK company name alongside the registered address; ensure exact punctuation (including "Ltd" vs. "Limited") matches Companies House filings character for character; and provide clear corporate website proof explaining remote software operations honestly.

4. Connecting Wise Business to Stripe UK Without Triggers

Once Wise Business local GBP receiving credentials are active, linking them inside Stripe UK takes minutes. Before launching paid traffic, minimize sudden Stripe risk holds by keeping public refund and terms pages clearly accessible, ensuring Stripe's registered company number and director identity match Companies House identically, and keeping payout descriptors transparent so international buyers recognize card charges without filing disputes.

From an operational accounting perspective, Wise Business simplifies multi-currency bookkeeping significantly: SaaS revenue settled in GBP from Stripe UK can stay in GBP for European or UK vendor spend, convert dynamically to USD at mid-market rates for cloud and AI API invoices (AWS, OpenAI, Anthropic, Cloudflare), or wire cleanly back to founder local accounts with complete audit trails—eliminating hidden double-conversion bank fees that silently erode 3% to 5% of early software margins.

5. Backup Banking Hygiene for Solo Global Founders

Even reliable fintech accounts occasionally trigger periodic source-of-funds or invoice checks when monthly SaaS volume jumps abruptly. Maintaining clean digital folders containing customer receipts, supplier invoices, SaaS subscription terms, and annual statutory filings—and keeping a secondary pre-verified payment gateway or Merchant of Record option ready—protects cash flow continuity across peak launch months. Documenting clear business descriptions on incoming client invoices, keeping public cancellation and support links reachable, archiving clean monthly Stripe reconciliation exports, and avoiding unrelated personal crypto or third-party transfers inside corporate Wise accounts further minimizes automated risk flags over long-term multi-currency operations.

FAQ

Does Wise Business charge a monthly subscription fee for UK companies?

No. Wise Business does not charge recurring monthly maintenance fees for UK accounts, charging instead a one-time setup fee for local account details plus transparent low-percentage conversion and transfer fees when moving currencies.

Can non-UK residents connect a UK Wise Business account to Stripe?

Yes. Provided your UK Limited Company is properly registered at Companies House, verified inside Wise Business, and passes Stripe UK corporate identity checks, Stripe payouts settle directly into your Wise Business GBP account details.

Why does Wise reject some UK virtual office address proofs?

Rejections usually happen when uploaded address invoices list only the founder's personal name instead of the legal UK company name, or when entered registered/trading address lines conflict with official Companies House public records.
