Revision note (paste in English):

> Version 1.1 (21 November 2026) corrects version 1.0. The 105,000-entry catalog figure and its 97.8% ungraded share describe the Agent Skills Hub catalog around 10 June 2026; version 1.0 gave them without a date. The revision dates them and adds the catalog count of 21 November 2026 (281,000+ entries, 142,000+ with a rule-based grade). Findings from the July 2026 snapshot now cite its dataset card. The title now names the snapshot the figures describe.
