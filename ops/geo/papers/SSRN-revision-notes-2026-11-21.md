## 1. Supply-chain security paper（SSRN abstract_id 7737258）

Revision note (paste in English):

> Version 1.1 (21 November 2026) corrects version 1.0. The 105,000-entry catalog figure and its 97.8% ungraded share describe the Agent Skills Hub catalog around 10 June 2026; version 1.0 gave them without a date. The revision dates them and adds the catalog count of 21 November 2026 (281,000+ entries, 142,000+ with a rule-based grade). It removes per-group counts that had been worked back from rounded percentages, stops describing the 2,300+ graded entries as a five-dimension audit (the five dimensions are a checklist used in a separate manual review of 200+ repositories), corrects a November 2026 count of graded entries that had been given as the number indexed, and removes a recurring pattern (typosquatted forks) that no data supports. Findings from the July 2026 snapshot now cite its dataset card. The title now names the snapshot the figures describe. Also archived on Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24420919

## 2. Payments paper（SSRN abstract_id 7737182）

- 上传：`ops/geo/papers/Zhu-2026-Indie-SaaS-Payments-MoR-Shift-Working-Paper.pdf`（版本 1.1，11 页）
- 新标题：Payment Platforms of New Online Sellers, 2023–2026: AI Sellers Make Up a Far Larger Share on Merchant of Record Platforms than on Stripe (Evidence from 200,000 Checkout Links and a $20 Cross-Border Subscription Fee Comparison)
- 旧标题：The Structural Shift from Payment Gateways to Merchant of Record Platforms Among Independent Software and AI Founders: Evidence from 200,000 Live Checkout Links and a $20 Cross-Border Subscription Fee Audit (2023–2026)
- 摘要：用 PDF 第一页的摘要原文。
- 作者联系邮箱同样改成了 jason@jasonzhu.ai。

Revision note (paste in English):

> Version 1.1 (22 November 2026) corrects version 1.0; no counts, shares or fees changed. Version 1.0, titled "The Structural Shift from Payment Gateways to Merchant of Record Platforms Among Independent Software and AI Founders", read the platform shares as AI founders preferring Merchant of Record platforms. The shares describe the mix of sellers within each platform: Stripe, being far larger, still has more AI sellers in absolute numbers (7% of 102,590 is about 7,200, against 2,354 new sellers of any kind on Dodo Payments, Polar and Creem combined). The title now says what the data show. The revision also states that Waffo Pancake's $20 total leaves out its 1% payout fee while other totals include one, dates the start of the 2026 rise in the AI share correctly, presents Paddle's undercount as an inference rather than a finding, credits Liu Xiaopai's published breakdown as the source of most fee rows, and describes the data archive as Zenodo rather than as a CERN-endorsed source. Also archived on Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24420870
