# 200,000 Indie Developer Checkout Links & Payment Platform Adoption Dataset (2023–2026)

**Author**: Jason Zhu ([jasonzhu.ai](https://jasonzhu.ai))

**Full Report & Methodology**: https://jasonzhu.ai/en/blog/indie-payments-report-2026

**8-Platform Comparison Guide**: https://jasonzhu.ai/en/blog/choose-payment-platform-2026

## Key Empirical Findings
- **Sample Size**: ~200,000 sellers across 198,000 unique websites linking to checkout pages on Stripe, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Creem, Dodo Payments, Paddle, and Waffo.
- **Growth Acceleration**: 21,000 new sellers in 2023, 29,000 in 2024, 56,000 in 2025, and 82,000 in 2026 (through October 15, 2026).
- **Software & AI Shift**: The share of new sellers shipping software quadrupled from 5% (2023) to 20% (September 2026); AI products rose from 2% to 12%.
- **MoR Clustering**: 24%–29% of new sellers on Dodo Payments (29%), Polar (27%), and Creem (24%) sell AI products, compared to just 7% on Stripe.
