Version 2, 21 November 2026. Compiled by Jason Zhu (ORCID 0009-0000-2894-6285). Six of the eight rows are from Liu Xiaopai's post of 15 November 2026.

## What this is

What one $20 monthly subscription, paid with an international card, costs the seller under eight payment setups, with recurring billing, sales tax handling and fraud screening switched on. The table lists each fee component, the amount taken, the amount kept, and whether a payout fee is included.

- Rows from Liu Xiaopai's post: Dodo Payments (partner rate), Waffo Pancake, other MoR platforms advertising 3.9%, Dodo Payments (default rate), Stripe Hong Kong, Lemon Squeezy. An English summary is at https://jasonzhu.ai/en/blog/dodo-payments-waffo-ai-saas-fee-comparison.
- Rows computed by Jason Zhu from published rates: Paddle and Polar, from the rates in https://jasonzhu.ai/en/blog/choose-payment-platform-2026 (platform pricing pages read on 15 October 2026). Their payout fees are not included.

Fees change. Check each platform's current pricing before relying on a row.

## Notes on individual rows

- **Payout fees are not counted the same way in every row.** See `payout_fee_in_total`. Waffo Pancake's $1.28 leaves out its 1% payout fee (minimum $10 per payout). The "3.9%" row and Lemon Squeezy include a 1% payout fee. Dodo Payments charges no payout fee on payouts of $1,000 or more and $5 below that.
- **Dodo Payments partner rate**: 3.8%–3.9% + $0.40 with no international or subscription fee, and the first $200 processed free. Liu Xiaopai negotiated it with Dodo Payments' CEO; it applies only to sellers who sign up through his invitation link. Everyone else pays the default rate (4% + $0.40, plus 1.5% for international cards and 0.5% for subscriptions).
- **"Other MoR platforms advertising 3.9%"**: the source does not name a platform. Creem's published terms on 15 October 2026 (3.9% + $0.40; payout fee 1% or 7 USD/EUR, whichever is higher) give the same $1.37.
- **Stripe Hong Kong** is not a Merchant of Record and requires a Hong Kong company. Its total assumes the payment is converted from USD to HKD (2%); paying out in USD instead costs 1%.
- **Lemon Squeezy** will stop processing payments on 31 March 2027.
